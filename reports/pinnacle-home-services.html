1 Executive Summary
68/100
Needs Work

Pinnacle Home Services has a solid SEO foundation in place — a clean keyword-targeted homepage title, individual service pages for slab and raised foundations, problem-signs landing pages, and a blog that already ranks for several informational queries. The site is HTTPS, mobile-friendly, and includes FAQ schema, which is exactly the kind of structured data Google rewards for foundation repair searches.

The biggest gaps are local visibility and content depth around how Sacramento homeowners actually search. There is no LocalBusiness schema (only FAQPage and BreadcrumbList), no city or neighborhood-specific landing pages beyond Sacramento and West Sacramento, and limited long-tail content around the exact problem-based phrases people Google before they call. Closing these would lift you above competitors like Stronghold and the franchise dealers in map-pack and organic results.
