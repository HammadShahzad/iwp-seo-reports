1 Executive Summary
68/100
Needs Work

Reliable Solutions Atlanta has built a strong content foundation — dedicated service pages, a deep blog, and seven service area pages give the site real structure most foundation repair competitors in Metro Atlanta lack. LocalBusiness schema is implemented and the brand voice (family-owned, 20+ years, BBB A+) comes through clearly. The site reads like a real operator's site, not a template.

The gaps are mostly technical polish. Title tags repeat the brand name twice on every service page, social sharing metadata is incomplete, one URL ships with a typo (/cocnrete-repair), and the Next.js rendering means a chunk of body content isn't visible to crawlers in static HTML. Fixing the meta layer and tightening on-page signals would lift visibility for high-intent searches like 'foundation repair Atlanta' and 'basement waterproofing cost Atlanta' without rebuilding anything.
