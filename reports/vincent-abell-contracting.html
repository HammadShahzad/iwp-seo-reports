1 Executive Summary
58/100
Critical

Vincent Abell Contracting has strong fundamentals — a 25+ year track record, a recognizable family name in Louisville, an active portfolio, and reviews on Houzz, Angie's, BBB, and GuildQuality. The site loads on HTTPS, the page titles include the city, and individual service pages are reasonably well-written with pricing context that homeowners actually want to see.

The big leaks are on the technical and on-page side. The homepage meta description and H1 don't position you for any of the searches homeowners actually run ("kitchen remodel Louisville," "general contractor Highlands," "home additions Louisville KY"), there's no LocalBusiness schema telling Google your service area, neighborhood-specific landing pages don't exist as standalone URLs, and the portfolio images are missing descriptive alt text and structured data. Fixing the on-page basics and adding 6–8 neighborhood pages would meaningfully move local rankings within 60–90 days.

6 Content Gap Recommendations

Topics and content types your competitors cover that you're missing.

Neighborhood landing pages for Louisville's premium remodeling areas High Priority

Homeowners in Highlands, Cherokee Triangle, and Saint Matthews search by neighborhood. You serve them but have no page targeting those phrases.

Recommended format: Six pages, ~600-800 words each, with neighborhood-specific projects and a service-area map

Written project case studies (not just photo galleries) High Priority

Google can't read a photo. Case studies with scope, timeline, budget range, and challenges turn portfolio pages into ranking content.

Recommended format: 8-10 detailed case studies linked from the portfolio with before/after photos and 400+ words of context

Cost guide content for Louisville remodeling High Priority

"Cost to remodel a kitchen in Louisville" and similar phrases have steady demand and high commercial intent — they convert.

Recommended format: Long-form blog posts (1,200+ words) with local cost ranges and an internal link to the matching service page

Homeowner-facing FAQ and process content Medium Priority

Builds trust and captures featured snippets for questions like 'how long does a kitchen remodel take?'

Recommended format: FAQ schema on service pages plus a dedicated 'Our Process' page

Reviews and testimonials surfaced on the site Medium Priority

You have 5-star reviews on Houzz, GuildQuality, BBB, and Angie's, but they live off-site. Pulling them in builds trust signals for Google and visitors.

Recommended format: A testimonials page plus quote callouts on each service page

Basement remodeling and load-bearing service pages Medium Priority

Currently only portfolio categories — they're missing the conversion-focused service pages that drive bookings.

Recommended format: Standalone service pages with scope, pricing context, and gallery
