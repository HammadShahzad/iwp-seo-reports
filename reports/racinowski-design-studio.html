1 Executive Summary
61/100
Needs Work

Racinowski Design Studio's portfolio is genuinely exceptional — award-winning mid-century work, features in Architectural Digest and Dezeen, nearly two decades of Milwaukee projects. The aesthetic and craft come through clearly when a visitor lands on the homepage. The problem is that Google has a much harder time seeing what your visitors see. The current Squarespace setup leaves a lot of search visibility on the table for terms like 'modern residential architect Milwaukee' and 'mid-century renovation Wisconsin' — keywords your portfolio absolutely deserves to rank for.

Three issues are doing most of the damage. First, your entire homepage navigation runs on anchor links (#projects, #about) rather than separate indexable pages — so Google can't treat your About or Projects index as a standalone page to rank. Second, project page images use raw filename alt text (e.g., 'tonyawatha_17 FIX LARGE.jpg') instead of descriptive captions, which is a missed opportunity given how heavily your site relies on imagery. Third, there's no LocalBusiness or Architect schema and almost no on-page mention of 'Milwaukee', 'Wisconsin', or 'Fox Point' in titles or meta descriptions — which makes local ranking unnecessarily hard for a firm that's been named Best Architect Milwaukee multiple times.
