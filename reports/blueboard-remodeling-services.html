1 Executive Summary
58/100
Critical

Blueboard Remodeling has a clean, modern site built on Elementor that loads quickly and reads well on mobile. The fundamentals are in place — HTTPS, sensible page titles, meta descriptions across the main service pages, and a clear list of services covering Boise and surrounding towns like Eagle, Meridian, Nampa, Caldwell, and Kuna.

Where the site is leaving real money on the table is in three areas: thin service pages that read like template copy rather than local expertise, a gallery that uses generic stock-style imagery with non-descriptive filenames, and a Gmail address as the primary contact rather than a branded inbox. Add a couple of city-specific landing pages, real project case studies, and structured review markup, and this site can comfortably outrank the small-shop competition in the Treasure Valley.

6Content Gap Recommendations

Topics and content types your competitors cover that you're missing.

Real local project case studies High Priority

Homeowners hiring a remodeler want to see real work in homes that look like theirs — stock photos hurt trust and Google can tell the difference

Recommended format: Case study posts with before/after photos, scope, timeline, budget range, and the neighborhood

City-specific service area pages High Priority

Meridian, Eagle, Nampa, Caldwell, and Kuna each have their own search volume — one combined page can't rank for all of them

Recommended format: One landing page per city, each with locally-specific intro, project examples, and FAQs

Cost / pricing guides High Priority

'Cost to remodel kitchen Boise' style searches have strong intent and pull people to the bottom of the funnel quickly

Recommended format: Pricing guide blog posts with low / mid / high budget ranges for Treasure Valley homes

Reviews and testimonials on-page High Priority

The current 'What Our Clients Say' section is empty — that's a major credibility gap on every page

Recommended format: Embedded Google reviews carousel + 2-3 written testimonials per service page

Process / what-to-expect page Medium Priority

First-time remodelers want to understand the process; this builds trust and answers objections before the consult

Recommended format: A 'How we work' page covering consult, design, permitting, build phases, and warranty

FAQ schema markup Medium Priority

FAQ content already exists on the homepage but is not marked up as FAQPage schema, so Google can't show it as rich results

Recommended format: Add JSON-LD FAQPage schema for the existing 8 FAQs
