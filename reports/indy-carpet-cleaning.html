1 Executive Summary

Needs Work

Indy Carpet Cleaning has built a solid foundation with a long-running, family-owned operation, transparent pricing, an active service menu, and HouseCallPro booking — all the pieces a strong local site needs. Page-level SEO is where things start to slip, with vague title tags, a meta description that contains grammatical errors, and a homepage canonical that points to a different URL than the one customers land on. These small inconsistencies confuse search engines and quietly cost rankings on the highest-value local terms.

The biggest growth lever is content and service-area depth. There are no individual landing pages for Carmel, Fishers, Greenwood, Zionsville and the other suburbs you serve, the blog has only one post in 2026 after a long quiet stretch, and the homepage carries thin, list-style copy that could rank for far more queries with focused topic pages. With targeted fixes to titles, meta data, schema, and a real service-area + blog content plan, this site can realistically climb several positions for the keywords that drive booked jobs.

6 Content Gap Recommendations

Topics and content types your competitors cover that you're missing.

Dedicated suburb pages (Carmel, Fishers, Greenwood, Zionsville, Westfield) High Priority

These are the highest-converting search terms and the easiest local wins — currently the site lists suburbs as text only

Recommended format: 5 landing pages, ~600-800 words each, with local reviews, before/after photos, and a map

Pet stain and odor removal guide High Priority

Pet stain searches are high-intent and convert well; current page is thin and lacks schema

Recommended format: Long-form guide (1,500+ words) + FAQ schema + linked to service page

Carpet cleaning pricing transparency page High Priority

Most local competitors hide prices — your upfront pricing is a differentiator that should rank for 'carpet cleaning prices indianapolis'

Recommended format: Dedicated pricing page with comparison table, FAQ schema, and CTAs

Move-out / property manager content Medium Priority

Property managers and landlords are repeat customers; this niche has lower competition than residential

Recommended format: Industry page targeting property managers + apartment turnovers

Seasonal carpet care content Medium Priority

Spring cleaning, holiday prep, and winter salt removal are predictable seasonal searches with low competition

Recommended format: Blog series — 4 seasonal posts per year, evergreen with refreshes

Carpet type / fiber care guides Low Priority

Educational content like 'how to clean wool carpet' captures top-of-funnel traffic that builds brand authority

Recommended format: Blog post series, internally linked to relevant service pages
