1 Executive Summary
64/100
Needs Work

Hayes Studio has 25+ years of beautiful Phoenix architecture and interior design work, multiple 'Best of' awards, and a wide portfolio across homes, hospitality, and commercial spaces. The site looks polished and the meta tags are well written — but the underlying Squarespace setup leaves a lot of search traffic on the table. The biggest issue is that the homepage acts more like a splash/landing screen than an indexable page: there is no clear H1, almost no body content, and most of the rich project work is buried two clicks deep.

The portfolio is the strongest asset on the site, and individual project pages exist — but they aren't being optimized for the keywords clients actually search for ('phoenix custom home architect', 'hospitality architect arizona', 'restaurant architect phoenix'). A handful of project links point to the old hayes-inc.squarespace.com subdomain rather than hayesstudio.com, which splits link equity. Fixing these structural items, adding a proper homepage H1 and intro copy, and writing dedicated service-area pages for Phoenix/Scottsdale/Paradise Valley would unlock meaningful organic traffic over the next 6 months.
