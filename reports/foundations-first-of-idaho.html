1 Executive Summary
64/100
Needs Work

Foundations First of Idaho has solid fundamentals — a clean Divi-built WordPress site, family-owned positioning with 40+ years of experience, and dedicated pages for most core services like foundation repair, crawl space repair, wall stabilization, sump pumps, French drains, and earthquake retrofitting. Schema markup is in place, the main service pages have unique titles and meta descriptions, and the homepage signals trust well with publications and certifications.

The biggest gaps holding the site back from ranking better in Boise, Meridian, and Nampa: 60%+ of homepage images are missing alt text, the homepage H1 is generic ("The Foundation Company in Idaho") rather than keyword-targeted for Boise searches, and there are no city-specific landing pages for Treasure Valley suburbs (Nampa, Caldwell, Eagle, Garden City). The blog is thin on the high-intent informational queries homeowners actually search before calling, and there's no dedicated content covering local Idaho-specific issues like clay-heavy soil settling, basalt foundations, or seismic retrofit cost.

6 Content Gap Recommendations

Topics and content types your competitors cover that you're missing.

Treasure Valley city service area pages High Priority

Searches like 'foundation repair Nampa' and 'foundation repair Meridian' have steady commercial intent but you have no dedicated landing pages. Each city page can rank independently.

Recommended format: Individual service area pages (/service-area/nampa/, /service-area/meridian/, etc.) with unique copy, local case studies, and embedded map

Foundation repair cost guide for Idaho homeowners High Priority

Cost is the single most-searched informational query before homeowners call a contractor. Transparency builds trust and earns mid-funnel traffic that converts well.

Recommended format: Long-form pillar page with cost ranges by repair type, factors that affect price, and a CTA for free estimate

Signs your home needs foundation repair (with photos) High Priority

High monthly search volume and the perfect top-of-funnel content for homeowners noticing cracks or sticking doors. Builds brand awareness before they're ready to buy.

Recommended format: Visual blog post with before/after photos, FAQ schema, and links to service pages

Earthquake retrofit guide for Boise homes Medium Priority

Boise sits in a seismic zone and retrofit interest spikes after every notable Idaho quake. You already do retrofitting but the page doesn't capture this seasonal demand.

Recommended format: Cornerstone content covering when retrofit is needed, cost ranges, the FFID process, and Idaho-specific code considerations

Idaho soil types and how they affect your foundation Medium Priority

Differentiates you as a local expert and earns links from local realtor and inspector sites. No competitor in Boise covers this well.

Recommended format: Educational guide with map graphics of Treasure Valley soil zones

Helical piers vs push piers — which is right for your home? Medium Priority

High-commercial-intent comparison query that converts well. You feature helical piles prominently but have no comparison content.

Recommended format: Comparison post with diagrams, pros/cons, and recommendation by foundation type

7 Prioritized Action Plan
Quick Wins do this week — under 2 hours each
  1. Add alt text to the 28 images missing it on the homepage — describe the foundation problem or repair shown
  2. Rewrite the homepage H1 to include 'Foundation Repair Boise & Meridian' instead of the generic current version
  3. Add FAQPage JSON-LD schema to your existing /frequently-asked-questions/ page to qualify for rich snippets

  6. Add internal links between related services (crawl space ↔ sump pump ↔ French drains ↔ waterproofing)
  7. Compress and lazy-load the hero images on the homepage to reduce time-to-interactive on mobile
  8. Add BreadcrumbList schema and visible breadcrumb navigation to all service pages
