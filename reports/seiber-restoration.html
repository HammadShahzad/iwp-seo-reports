1 Executive Summary
64/100
Needs Work

Seiber Restoration has a clear identity advantage in the Knoxville market — veteran-owned, locally operated, and focused on foundation repair and waterproofing in a region with notoriously challenging clay soil. The current site does a good job conveying trust on the homepage with reviews, mission statement, and a clear service overview, and the main service pages for Foundations and Waterproofing are well written. Where the site is leaving money on the table is in search visibility: when a Knoxville homeowner Googles things like "foundation repair Knoxville" or "bowed basement wall repair near me," your site has real trouble showing up because the on-page SEO foundations (no pun intended) and local SEO signals are underbuilt.

Three big gaps explain most of the score. First, the homepage title tag is generic and doesn't mention Knoxville or the city you serve — a baseline miss for local search. Second, several pages have duplicate H1 tags and weak meta descriptions, which hurts how Google interprets each page's topic. Third, there is no LocalBusiness schema markup and no individual service-area pages (Farragut, Maryville, Sevierville, Oak Ridge, etc.), which is the single biggest lever for ranking in nearby towns. Fix these and you'll start showing up for the exact searches homeowners use right before they call a contractor.

6 Content Gap Recommendations

Topics and content types your competitors cover that you're missing.

"Signs your home has foundation problems" — symptoms guide High Priority

Homeowners search symptom-based queries (cracks in walls, sticking doors, uneven floors) before they search for contractors. Capturing this traffic builds trust and feeds your service pages.

Recommended format: Long-form blog post (1,500+ words) with photos of each warning sign + FAQ schema

Foundation repair cost in Knoxville — pricing guide High Priority

"Cost" is one of the most searched modifiers for any service. A transparent cost range guide with East Tennessee context will pull strong local traffic.

Recommended format: Cost guide page with ranges by repair type + a contact CTA

Service area pages (Farragut, Maryville, Oak Ridge, Sevierville) High Priority

Each surrounding town is a separate ranking opportunity. Competitors already have these pages, you don't.

Recommended format: Individual 600–800 word pages per town with local references, service list, contact info

Crawl space encapsulation — dedicated service page High Priority

Crawl space work is a high-margin service with strong search volume in East Tennessee. Currently buried inside the Waterproofing page.

Recommended format: Dedicated service page with process steps, benefits, photos, and FAQ

Carbon fiber wall reinforcement vs steel braces — comparison Medium Priority

Homeowners researching bowed wall repair will compare methods. Owning this comparison builds authority and pre-qualifies leads.

Recommended format: Educational blog post with diagrams + decision flowchart

How long does foundation repair take? Project timelines Medium Priority

Captures top-of-funnel informational searches and reduces sales-call friction.

Recommended format: FAQ-style blog post with timeline tables for each repair type

7 Prioritized Action Plan
Quick Wins do this week — under 2 hours each
  1. Rewrite the homepage title tag to lead with "Foundation Repair & Waterproofing in Knoxville, TN" and end with the brand
  2. Add a 150–160 character meta description to the homepage
  3. Fix the duplicate H1 tags on the Waterproofing and The Seiber Difference pages (demote the second H1 to H2)
  4. Add the missing alt text on the one image on the homepage that has an empty alt attribute
  5. Add an FAQ section to the homepage with FAQPage schema covering 6–8 common foundation questions
  6. Add LocalBusiness schema markup with address, hours, phone, service area, and Google Maps coordinates
  7. Verify Google Business Profile is fully optimized (services list, photos, posts) and matches website NAP exactly
  8. Submit an updated XML sitemap to Google Search Console and double-check no service pages are blocked in robots.txt
Strategic Investments plan for this quarter
  1. Build out 8–10 individual service pages — pier installation, helical piers, carbon fiber straps, wall braces, crack injection, crawl space encapsulation, sump pumps, mudjacking, tuckpointing — each targeting one keyword phrase

  5. Set up a review generation system that consistently asks customers for Google reviews — closing the gap from 20 to 100+ reviews is the single biggest local-ranking lever
  6. Add proper FAQPage and Service schema across all pages so Google can surface FAQ snippets and service rich results
