1 Executive Summary
54/100
Critical

Studio MAYO has a beautifully built portfolio — the Bernheim H2Oh! land sculpture, the customizable ADU tool kit, the Cana Island interpretive center — work that should be ranking when people search for modern, sustainable, or ADU architects in Louisville. But the website is leaving most of that visibility on the table. The homepage has zero H1 headings, no H2 structure, and the title tag is over 130 characters long, so Google truncates it before reaching the keywords that matter. The canonical link still points to the http:// version of the site even though the real site auto-redirects to https://, which sends mixed signals to crawlers.

The bigger gap is on the portfolio pages, which is where an architecture firm's SEO should actually live. Project pages like Cabin and Pool House have no meta descriptions, no headings, no body copy, and the image alt text is just filenames ("9+MEADOW+FALL.jpg"). That means individual project pages can't rank for the searches they should own — "modern cabin architect Kentucky," "ADU architect Louisville," "adaptive reuse architect." Add slow load times (9.5 seconds, with a single 4.1MB image on the homepage) and you have a portfolio that looks fantastic to humans but is mostly invisible to search engines. The good news: every issue here is fixable inside Squarespace without rebuilding the site.
