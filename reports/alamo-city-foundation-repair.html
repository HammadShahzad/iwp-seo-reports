1 Executive Summary
52/100
Critical

Alamo City Foundation Repair has a clean, modern website and a genuinely strong real-world reputation — 30+ years of experience under owner Jack Martin, a 5-star Google rating, and an A+ BBB rating. The raw material for online growth is all here. The gap is that the website doesn't yet reflect that reputation in a way search engines can read, understand, and rank.

The site currently runs on just a handful of pages, with all services bundled onto the homepage rather than given their own dedicated pages. There is no informational content, and page titles are generic — they don't mention San Antonio or the specific services homeowners search for. A few small technical issues, including broken contact links and missing image descriptions, are also costing easy points. Fixing these would help the site surface when local homeowners search for foundation help.

6 Content Gap Recommendations

Topics and content types your competitors cover that you're missing.

'Signs your foundation needs repair' homeowner guide High Priority

One of the highest-volume informational searches in this industry; it builds trust and pulls in homeowners early, before they have chosen a contractor

Recommended format: Blog post with photos + FAQ schema

Individual service pages for each repair type High Priority

Pier and beam, concrete repair, masonry, drainage and retaining walls are each searched separately; one bundled homepage section cannot rank for all of them

Recommended format: Dedicated service pages, one per service

'Foundation repair cost in San Antonio' guide High Priority

Cost is the question homeowners search most before calling; a transparent guide captures that high-intent traffic

Recommended format: Pricing guide page with ranges and FAQs

Service area pages for nearby communities Medium Priority
