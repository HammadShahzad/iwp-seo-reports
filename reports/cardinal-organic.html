1 Executive Summary
58/100
Critical

Cardinal Organic has a genuinely strong story to tell — IICRC-certified, eco-friendly Very Low Moisture cleaning, one client per day, and transparent pricing. Those are real differentiators in a crowded Charlotte market. But the website is undermining that story in a few critical ways: the phone numbers don't match across the page (the click-to-call link routes to a Las Vegas number), the email link is broken, the meta description is missing, and the homepage is one long wall of text instead of guided sections. Search engines and visitors both end up confused.

On the SEO side, the biggest wins are quick: fix the broken NAP (name/address/phone) consistency, add LocalBusiness schema, write a real meta description, and split out dedicated city pages for Waxhaw, Ballantyne, Fort Mill, and Matthews — those are searches you should already be ranking for. With a focused six-week effort you could realistically move from page 2 to top 3 for several of your highest-intent local terms, especially the niche organic and VLM searches where you face almost no direct competition.

7 Prioritized Action Plan
Quick Wins do this week — under 2 hours each
  1. Fix the broken click-to-call link — it currently routes to a Las Vegas number (area code 702). Pick one phone number and use it consistently across the entire site.
  2. Fix the email link in the footer — it's currently linked as http://g@cardinalorganic.com instead of mailto:g@cardinalorganic.com so clicks go nowhere.
  3. Write a real meta description for the homepage and top 5 service pages — keep each under 155 characters and include 'Charlotte' or your city.
  4. Update the footer copyright from 2018 to 2026 (use a dynamic year so this never falls behind again).
  5. Remove or substantially shorten the COVID-19 paragraphs — they're dated and push your real differentiators below the fold.
  6. Add a single canonical phone number to your Google Business Profile, Facebook, and BBB listing so Google sees consistent NAP.
  7. Add LocalBusiness JSON-LD schema to the homepage with hours, address, phone, service area, price range, and review aggregate.
  8. Compress the largest PNG images on the site — converting decorative graphics to WebP can cut page weight 40-60%.
  9. Add descriptive alt text to all images on the services page — currently many are blank or generic file names.
Strategic Investments plan for this quarter
  1. Build dedicated service-area landing pages for your top 6-8 cities (Waxhaw, Ballantyne, Matthews, Fort Mill, Indian Trail, Weddington, Huntersville, Rock Hill). Each page should include locally-relevant content, embedded map, and testimonials from that area.
  2. Launch a focused content program: publish one practical, search-optimized blog post per month on topics like 'VLM vs Steam Cleaning,' 'Pet Urine Removal,' and 'Carpet Cleaning Frequency.' This builds authority and informational traffic.
  3. Redesign the homepage hero to lead with your three real differentiators: organic VLM cleaning, IICRC-certified, one client per day. Move COVID content off the homepage entirely.
  4. Build out the urine decontamination and odor remediation services into full standalone pages with before/after galleries, FAQs, and clear pricing — these are high-margin services with low local competition.
  5. Implement a review-collection system: after each job, send a one-tap email/SMS asking happy customers to review on Google. Aim for 2-3 new Google reviews per month — this directly drives Google Maps rankings.
  6. Establish a citation-building program to ensure your name, address, and phone are consistent across Google Business Profile, Yelp, BBB, Facebook, Apple Maps, and 15-20 industry directories. Inconsistent NAP is currently your biggest local SEO drag.
  7. Add a transparent pricing/quote calculator to your site — your honest pricing is a real competitive advantage that almost no competitor offers.
