1 Executive Summary
58/100
Critical

Cardinal Organic has built a strong local reputation in the Charlotte / Waxhaw area with a clear point of differentiation — Very Low Moisture cleaning, IICRC certification, eco-friendly products, and a one-client-per-day model. The website communicates that story to anyone who lands on it, and customer testimonials reinforce it well.

The bigger challenge is getting people to land on the site in the first place. Page titles, meta descriptions, heading structure and on-page targeting are all built for a visitor who already knows the brand — not for someone in Ballantyne searching "carpet cleaning near me." Add inconsistent NAP information (two different phone numbers across pages), missing LocalBusiness schema, an outdated 2018 copyright in the footer, and no dedicated service-area pages for the eleven cities listed, and there is meaningful organic search opportunity being left on the table.

Your entire differentiator is VLM. Most prospects don't know what that is or why it matters — educational content closes that gap and ranks

7 Prioritized Action Plan
Quick Wins do this week — under 2 hours each
  1. Rewrite the homepage title tag to include "Carpet Cleaning Charlotte NC" plus your differentiator (VLM / eco-friendly)
  2. Write a focused 150-character meta description for the homepage with primary keyword and a CTA
  3. Pick one phone number and make it identical across the homepage, About page, footer, and Google Business Profile
  4. Update the footer copyright from 2018 to the current year (dynamic year via PHP date())
  5. Consolidate the homepage down to a single H1 and demote the rest to H2
  6. Add LocalBusiness schema (JSON-LD) with name, address, phone, service area, hours, geo coordinates
  7. Rewrite image alt text on the homepage so it describes the service or location, not just the object
  8. Add a clear "Areas We Serve" section with anchor links — sets up the future city pages
Strategic Investments plan for this quarter
  1. Build 11 dedicated service-area pages (Waxhaw, Charlotte, Weddington, Ballantyne, Matthews, Indian Trail, Fort Mill, Rock Hill, Tega Cay, Marvin, Lancaster) — each 600-800 words with localized copy and at least one local review
  2. Launch a real blog calendar with at least one post per month focused on long-tail informational keywords (drying times, pet urine, VLM, eco-friendly)
  3. Build out a proper pet urine decontamination service page with case studies and FAQs — it's high-margin and high-intent
  4. Run a citation cleanup across NAP listings (Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Facebook, BBB, HomeGuide) so all phone numbers and addresses match the canonical version
  5. Get review velocity moving — a structured ask after every job, with direct GBP review links sent by SMS the same evening
  6. Compress and serve images as WebP, defer non-critical Elementor JS, and target a Lighthouse mobile score north of 80
