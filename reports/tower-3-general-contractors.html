1 Executive Summary
54/100
Critical

Tower 3 has built a strong portfolio of historic renovations, additions, and kitchen/bath remodels in Richmond since 2015, but the website is leaving a lot of that credibility hidden from Google. The biggest red flag is that the entire site still loads over HTTP instead of HTTPS, which Chrome flags as Not Secure in the address bar and which Google has used as a ranking signal for years. Beyond that, the homepage title is just the company name, project pages have no meta descriptions, and image alt text reads like a list of keywords rather than a description of the photo.

The good news: the foundation is solid. The portfolio is genuinely impressive, the brand is distinctive, and there are real service categories (additions, historic renovation, kitchen, bath, new build) that map cleanly to high-intent local searches. Tightening the on-page basics, switching to HTTPS, and adding a few service area pages targeting Henrico, Chesterfield, and Hanover counties could move the site from invisible to competitive within a couple of months.

6 Content Gap Recommendations

Topics and content types your competitors cover that you're missing.

Dedicated service pages (Kitchen, Bath, Additions, Historic Renovation, New Build) High Priority

Right now everything funnels through one homepage. Google needs distinct pages to rank for distinct services, and prospects searching 'kitchen remodel Richmond' won't find a relevant landing page on your site.

Recommended format: 5 service pages, 500-800 words each, with 3-5 project photos and a clear call-to-action

Service area pages for Henrico, Chesterfield, Hanover, Goochland High Priority

Competitor AHG lists 10+ counties in their title tag for a reason — local search rewards specific geo targeting. You're losing searches in every county outside the city limits.

Recommended format: Short page per county (300-500 words) with a project from that area if possible

Cost guides — 'Kitchen remodel cost in Richmond', 'Home addition cost in Virginia' High Priority

These are the top informational searches homeowners run before they ever pick up the phone. Ranking for them earns you trust before the competitive sales conversation even starts.

Recommended format: Long-form blog posts (1,200-1,800 words) with real price ranges and your own project examples

Project case studies with written narrative Medium Priority

Your portfolio is your strongest asset but right now it's photos only. A 200-word write-up per project triples the SEO surface area and gives buyers the story they actually want to read.

Recommended format: Add a description block to each existing project page covering scope, challenges, materials, and outcome

About / Process / Design-Build page Medium Priority

Buyers researching a 6-figure renovation want to know how you work before they fill out a form. A clear process page reduces friction and earns long-tail search traffic for 'design build Richmond'.

Recommended format: Single page walking through consultation -> design -> build -> handoff with photos

Customer testimonials / reviews section Medium Priority

No social proof is visible on the homepage. Embedding 4-6 testimonials with names and neighborhoods builds trust and creates fresh keyword content.

Recommended format: Homepage testimonial carousel plus a dedicated Testimonials page with Review schema
