1 Executive Summary
51/100
Critical

Benak Builders has a clean, friendly site that tells a genuinely memorable story — a family team of current and retired Omaha firefighters who handle design in-house. That trust angle is a real advantage. The problem is that almost none of it is written in a way Google can read, so the site is largely invisible when Omaha homeowners search for a remodeler or general contractor.

The biggest gaps are thin on-page text, portfolio galleries with no descriptions or image alt text, no service-area pages for Omaha neighborhoods, and no LocalBusiness structured data. These are fixable without a rebuild, and addressing them would help the site start showing up for the kitchen, bath, basement, and new-construction searches that already match what Benak Builders does best.

6 Content Gap Recommendations

Topics and content types your competitors cover that you're missing.

Per-service pages (kitchen, bath, basement, decks, new construction) High Priority

Each is a search people actually type; one combined portfolio page can't rank for all of them

Recommended format: Individual service pages with photos, copy, and a clear CTA

Project case studies with descriptions High Priority

Galleries with captions and alt text build trust and give Google text to index

Recommended format: Before/after write-ups under each portfolio category

Omaha service-area content Medium Priority

Helps you show up for neighborhood and 'near me' searches

Recommended format: A service-area section listing Omaha + surrounding communities

Cost & how-to guides Medium Priority

Captures homeowners researching before they hire

Recommended format: Short blog posts (remodel costs, choosing a contractor)

Reviews / testimonials on-page Medium Priority

The testimonials section is currently empty; social proof drives conversions

Recommended format: Embedded Google reviews or quoted client testimonials

7 Prioritized Action Plan
Quick Wins do this week — under 2 hours each
  1. Add a unique title tag and meta description to the Portfolio and Our Process pages
  2. Write descriptive alt text for every portfolio and gallery image
  3. Change the homepage H1 to a search-relevant headline mentioning Omaha remodeling
  4. Add a few sentences of real text to the homepage describing core services and service area
  5. Fill in the empty testimonials section with 2-3 client quotes or a Google reviews embed
  6. Link the site to your Google Business Profile and keep name/address/phone consistent
Strategic Investments plan for this quarter
  1. Build a dedicated, optimized page for each core service (kitchen, bath, basement, decks, new construction)
  2. Add LocalBusiness / GeneralContractor schema markup across the site
  3. Turn portfolio categories into case studies with written project descriptions
  4. Create an Omaha service-area section and a short blog with cost and how-to guides
  5. Consider migrating off the GoDaddy builder to a platform with fuller SEO and speed control
