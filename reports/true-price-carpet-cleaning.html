1 Executive Summary
56/100
Critical

True Price has a solid local foundation — family-owned since 2020, clear service offerings, a recognizable brand, and a service area that covers Tampa Bay, St. Pete, Wesley Chapel, New Port Richey, and Apollo Beach. The bones of the site are reasonable: HTTPS is in place, the mobile viewport is set, and the basic navigation is clear. But the site is leaving real money on the table because of a few details that are quietly working against it in search.

The biggest issues are content quality and freshness signals: several service pages have auto-spun text with typos ("Carpeting Cleaning"), at least one page is using placeholder lorem-ipsum copy in its social-share description, and the footer still reads 2021 — all of which signal to both Google and visitors that the site is stale. Combined with the lack of LocalBusiness schema, missing neighborhood-specific service pages, and a service URL structure under /portfolio/, there's a clear opportunity to climb in the Tampa-area rankings with targeted, low-risk fixes.

7 Prioritized Action Plan
Quick Wins do this week — under 2 hours each
  1. Replace the lorem-ipsum Open Graph description ('Uncover the 5-century history...') on the Tampa Carpet Cleaning page with a real one-sentence summary
  2. Update the footer copyright to the current year (and switch to a dynamic year so it stays fresh)
  3. Fix the typos in the Tampa Carpet Cleaning service-page body copy ('Carpeting Cleaning' → 'Carpet Cleaning', remove the duplicated 'rejuvenated as well as rejuvenated')
  4. Rewrite the homepage H1 to include 'Carpet Cleaning in Tampa, FL' instead of just the brand name
  5. Add a LocalBusiness JSON-LD block in the footer with NAP, hours, service area, phone, and aggregate Google rating
  6. Improve image alt text on hero photos to describe the actual service and city (e.g. 'Carpet steam cleaning in a Tampa living room' rather than generic phrases)
  7. Publish one new blog post — even a short 400-word one — to break the 2023 silence and signal an active site
  8. Add Open Graph and Twitter card metadata that reflects the actual page (most pages currently share a generic image and copy)
Strategic Investments plan for this quarter
  1. Build five neighborhood service pages (Wesley Chapel, New Port Richey, Carrollwood, Apollo Beach, St. Petersburg) — this is the single biggest local-SEO lever you have
  2. Rewrite all six core service pages by hand in a natural voice — drop the auto-spun copy, add real customer language, target one primary keyword per page
  3. Migrate service URLs from /portfolio/ to /services/ with proper 301 redirects so the URLs match the intent
  4. Launch a simple before/after photo gallery and embed 3-4 photos on each service page
  5. Add an FAQ page with structured-data markup for common Tampa carpet-cleaning questions (drying time, pet safety, frequency, pricing)
  6. Set up a quarterly content cadence — one helpful blog post every 4-6 weeks — to keep the site signaling activity to Google
  7. Connect Google Business Profile reviews to the site and surface a live review widget on the homepage and contact page
  8. Audit and update the Elementor build — outdated page-builder versions can leak speed and accessibility wins
