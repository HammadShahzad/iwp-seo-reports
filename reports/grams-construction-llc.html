1 Executive Summary
58/100
Critical

Grams Construction has a solid foundation as a 15+ year established business in San Antonio with BBB accreditation, real social proof, and a recognizable local presence. The site loads cleanly and the messaging around metal buildings and concrete services is reasonably clear. From a visitor standpoint, someone who lands on the homepage gets the gist of what you do.

The bigger opportunity is in helping Google understand and rank you for the searches San Antonio homeowners and commercial owners are typing in every day. The homepage uses generic taglines like 'Built to Last' as its main heading instead of keyword-rich phrases like 'General Contractor in San Antonio.' There's no LocalBusiness or Contractor schema, the gallery page has placeholder text ('Slide title', 'Write your caption here') still showing, and the service lineup on your site only covers two categories even though you mention broader work. Closing these gaps should noticeably improve how often you show up in local search results.

6 Content Gap Recommendations

Topics and content types your competitors cover that you're missing.

Dedicated service pages for each concrete application High Priority

You list driveways, patios, RV pads, retaining walls — each is its own search query. Right now all sit on one page, competing for attention.

Recommended format: Individual service pages with photos, FAQs, and pricing ranges

Project portfolio with real case studies High Priority

Photos with location names and 'before/after' details help with both trust and long-tail SEO. The current gallery has placeholder text instead.

Recommended format: Filterable portfolio page with 1-paragraph write-up per project

Service area pages for San Antonio neighborhoods Medium Priority

Searches like 'concrete contractor Stone Oak' or 'general contractor Alamo Heights' are easier to rank for than the citywide term.

Recommended format: Short location-specific landing pages (250-400 words each)

Pricing guides and cost articles Medium Priority

Homeowners search 'cost of...' before they call. Capturing this earlier in the journey gets you in front of more leads.

Recommended format: Long-form blog posts with cost ranges, factors, and FAQs

Reviews / Testimonials page with schema High Priority

You're BBB-accredited and have a Yelp presence — surfacing those reviews on-site builds trust and enables Google rich snippets.

Recommended format: Dedicated page pulling reviews from Google/BBB/Yelp with Review schema markup

Commercial vs. residential split content Medium Priority

You serve both, but the messaging is mixed. Visitors looking for either don't get a tailored experience.

Recommended format: Two distinct sections or hub pages, each with relevant case studies
