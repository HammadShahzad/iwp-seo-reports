1 Executive Summary
58/100
Critical

Heartland Carpet & Upholstery Care has real, hard-earned advantages most local competitors don't: 25+ years of hands-on experience, CRI Seal of Approval certification, a 4.7-star Google rating, and a clear family-business story. The site does a good job conveying expertise once a visitor lands on it. The challenge is that the site is not doing enough to bring those visitors in from search in the first place — the structure, on-page optimization, and local SEO signals are leaving a lot of organic visibility on the table.

Three things stand out. First, page coverage is too thin — there are only two service pages, and no dedicated pages for tile & grout, area rugs, pet odor, or for the suburbs Heartland actually serves (Hilliard, Dublin, Westerville, Powell, Upper Arlington, Worthington, Bexley, Reynoldsburg). Second, on-page basics like H1 keyword use, meta description length, and image alt text are underused. Third, the site lacks LocalBusiness schema, FAQ schema, and fresh content — last service page update is from 2021. Fixing these would unlock meaningful organic traffic without changing how the business operates.

6 Content Gap Recommendations

Topics and content types your competitors cover that you're missing.

Tile & Grout Cleaning Service Page High Priority

Search demand is steady, and most local competitors already rank for this. Easy add-on revenue for an existing crew.

Recommended format: Service page, 700–900 words, with photos and pricing

Pet Stain & Urine Odor Removal Page High Priority

Heartland already offers urine treatment as an add-on, but it has no dedicated page. This is one of the highest-intent local queries.

Recommended format: Dedicated service page + before/after photos + FAQ

Service Area Pages for Columbus Suburbs High Priority

Hilliard, Dublin, Westerville, Powell, Upper Arlington, Worthington — these are searched as 'carpet cleaning [city]'. Competitors capture this. You don't.

Recommended format: 6–8 lightweight pages, ~400 words each, with local references

Area Rug & Oriental Rug Cleaning Medium Priority

Higher-margin service, often handled separately from wall-to-wall carpet. Currently invisible on the site.

Recommended format: Service page with care guidance + pickup/dropoff vs. in-home info

Stain Removal Guide Blog Series Medium Priority

Informational queries ('how to remove [stain] from carpet') drive consistent organic traffic and build topical authority.

Recommended format: 8–12 blog posts, ~500 words each, with photos

Transparent Pricing Page Medium Priority

Family Value Cleaning leads with '$40/area' and '$150 for 3 rooms' — visible pricing reduces friction. Your site asks visitors to call without anchoring any price.

Recommended format: Single page with starting prices, what's included, and add-ons

FAQ Page with FAQPage Schema Medium Priority

You have a FAQ page already but no schema. Adding FAQ schema lets Google surface answers directly in search results.

Recommended format: Update existing FAQ page + add JSON-LD FAQPage markup

Commercial / Office Carpet Cleaning Page Low Priority

Currently the site is residential-leaning. A commercial page would open up a separate B2B keyword set.

Recommended format: Dedicated commercial page with use cases

7 Prioritized Action Plan
Quick Wins do this week — under 2 hours each
  1. Rewrite the homepage H1 to include 'Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning' + 'Grove City' or 'Columbus' so the most important page is keyword-relevant.
  2. Expand the homepage meta description to ~150 characters with location + value proposition + 'CRI Certified'.
  3. Add LocalBusiness JSON-LD schema to the site footer (NAP, hours, service area, aggregateRating from Google reviews).
  4. Add FAQPage schema to the existing FAQ page — this is a 30-minute change that can win featured snippets.
  5. Update OpenGraph image URLs from http:// to https:// to avoid mixed-content issues on social shares.
  6. Audit all service-page images and add descriptive alt text using 'carpet cleaning' + location keywords.
  7. Add the full NAP (Name, Address, Phone) to the sitewide footer so it appears on every page.
  8. Sort the embedded reviews newest-first — visitors currently see 2015–2017 reviews before more recent ones.
  9. Refresh the 'last updated' content on the carpet cleaning service page so search engines see fresh signals.
  10. Add internal links from the homepage to upcoming service pages and from each service page back to the homepage and contact page.
Strategic Investments plan for this quarter
  1. Build 4–6 new dedicated service pages: Tile & Grout Cleaning, Pet Stain & Urine Removal, Area Rug Cleaning, Commercial Cleaning, Water Damage Restoration (if offered), Stain Protection Treatment.
  2. Roll out 6–8 service-area pages for Columbus suburbs: Hilliard, Dublin, Westerville, Powell, Upper Arlington, Worthington, Bexley, Reynoldsburg — each linked from a parent 'Areas We Serve' page.
  3. Launch a small content hub / blog with one post per month: stain-removal guides, 'how often should you clean carpets', 'what to expect from steam cleaning', etc. Target informational keywords.
  4. Add a transparent pricing page with starting prices, what's included, and how add-ons are quoted — even directional numbers will reduce drop-off.
  5. Implement a recent-reviews module that pulls fresh Google reviews automatically and displays the latest ones first, with aggregateRating schema.
  6. Run a Core Web Vitals + page-speed audit and compress/lazy-load the larger images; switch where possible to WebP.
  7. Set up Google Search Console + GA4 tracking and submit an updated XML sitemap so all the new pages get crawled quickly.
  8. Consider a light visual refresh — the brand and reputation are strong, but the design feels older than the work the business actually delivers.
