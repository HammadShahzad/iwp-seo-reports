1 Executive Summary
58/100
Critical

Pannell Construction has a clean, image-forward Squarespace site with a strong portfolio of recognizable Knoxville commercial projects like Kern's Bakery, Black Dog Brewhouse, and Smoothie King. The brand presentation is solid and trustworthy, and the site already includes LocalBusiness schema, which is a great foundation. However, several core SEO fundamentals are missing that are likely keeping the site from ranking for the searches Knoxville homeowners and small business owners actually type into Google.

The biggest gap is that the homepage has no H1 heading tag at all, meaning Google has no clear signal about what the page is primarily about. There's also no dedicated service pages for kitchen remodels, bath renovations, or commercial build-outs (each service is just a bullet point on the About page), and no service area pages for nearby cities like Farragut, Oak Ridge, Maryville, or Sevierville. Combined with slow page load (~4 seconds to interactive) and missing alt text on portfolio images, these fixes alone could meaningfully improve search visibility within a quarter.

6 Content Gap Recommendations

Topics and content types your competitors cover that you're missing.

Dedicated service pages for each offering High Priority

Right now Google has no specific page to rank when someone searches 'kitchen remodel Knoxville' or 'commercial build out Knoxville' — these are exactly the searches that drive qualified leads

Recommended format: One page per service with photos, process, FAQ, and a clear CTA

Service area pages for surrounding cities High Priority

Farragut, Oak Ridge, Maryville, and Sevierville all have homeowners searching for contractors — without area-specific pages you're invisible for those local searches

Recommended format: Short city-specific landing pages featuring relevant past projects and a local quote form

Project case studies with written narrative High Priority

Beautiful project photos exist but no story behind them; written case studies build trust and rank for niche searches like 'restaurant build out contractor Tennessee'

Recommended format: 200-400 word write-ups per project covering scope, timeline, challenges, and outcome

Customer testimonials and Google review integration Medium Priority

Trust signals are critical for high-ticket construction decisions, and review schema can earn rich snippets in search results

Recommended format: Testimonial section on homepage plus AggregateRating schema

Cost guides and process FAQs (blog) Medium Priority

Homeowners search 'how much does a kitchen remodel cost in Knoxville' constantly — owning these answers builds top-of-funnel authority

Recommended format: Short, evergreen blog posts (600-900 words each) with local data

Pricing or budget transparency page Low Priority

Competitors that share even rough pricing ranges build trust and pre-qualify leads, saving time on consultations

Recommended format: Single page with typical project budget tiers and what's included
