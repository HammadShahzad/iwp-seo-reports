1 Executive Summary
72/100
Needs Work

McCoy Contractors has built a genuinely strong foundation online — pun intended. The site is fast, mobile-friendly, secure, and has a clean structure with dedicated pages for each foundation crack type and seepage area. With 70 years of history and 10,000+ repairs to point to, the trust signals are already there. Most local competitors don't have nearly this much service-page depth.

The biggest opportunity isn't fixing what's broken — it's filling the gaps. The site is heavily focused on cracks and seepage but doesn't deeply cover topics homeowners actually search for: 'foundation repair cost Milwaukee,' bowing wall solutions, helical pier installation, crawl space encapsulation, or signs of foundation problems. The 'Basement Foundation Repairs Milwaukee' phrase is also repeated unnaturally on key pages, which can read as keyword stuffing to Google. A handful of content additions plus tightening up on-page copy would push this site noticeably higher in search.

6 Content Gap Recommendations

Topics and content types your competitors cover that you're missing.

Foundation Repair Cost Guide for Milwaukee Homeowners High Priority

'Foundation repair cost' is one of the highest-volume homeowner searches and you currently don't address it at all — competitors who do capture this traffic at the research stage

Recommended format: Long-form pillar page with price ranges by repair type, factors that affect cost, and a sample inspection process

Signs of Foundation Problems — Homeowner's Guide High Priority

Homeowners search for symptoms (sticking doors, sloped floors, wall cracks) long before searching for 'foundation repair company.' Capturing that traffic builds trust early

Recommended format: Visual blog post with photos of each warning sign, severity levels, and when to call a pro

Crawl Space Repair & Encapsulation High Priority

You mention crawl spaces in marketing but have no dedicated service page — competitors rank ahead for crawl space terms

Recommended format: Full service page covering moisture barriers, encapsulation, structural support, and pricing

Bowing Basement Walls — Repair Methods Compared Medium Priority

Bowing walls is a distinct, high-intent search query — your current foundation repair page bundles it with cracks instead of capturing this term

Recommended format: Standalone page comparing carbon fiber straps, wall anchors, steel beams with photos

Pier Systems Explained (Helical vs. Push vs. Steel) Medium Priority

Most local competitors don't have detailed pier system content — easy ranking opportunity for higher-ticket repair searches

Recommended format: Educational explainer with diagrams and project examples for each pier type

How Long Does Foundation Repair Take in Milwaukee? Medium Priority

Long-tail informational query with strong purchase intent — gives you another entry point for organic traffic

Recommended format: Short article with realistic timelines by repair type, plus a featured project case study

7 Prioritized Action Plan
Quick Wins do this week — under 2 hours each
  1. Remove the <meta keywords> tag from every page — outdated and not helping
  2. Rewrite the foundation repair service page to reduce 'Basement Foundation Repairs Milwaukee' repetition
  3. Add LocalBusiness schema to the homepage with NAP, hours, geo coordinates, and review data
  4. Add FAQPage schema to existing FAQ sections to win 'People Also Ask' placements
  5. Audit the 19+ service area pages and add at least 2-3 paragraphs of unique local content to each
  6. Add internal links from the homepage to the FAQs page and gallery to spread authority
  7. Update the H1 on the homepage so it doesn't conflict with the Brookfield-heavy body copy
Strategic Investments plan for this quarter
  1. Build a content hub with 10-15 evergreen articles (cost guides, warning signs, repair method comparisons) — biggest gap vs. competitors

  3. Create standalone pages for each repair method: helical piers, push piers, carbon fiber straps, wall anchors
  4. Develop a Milwaukee Foundation Repair Cost Guide as a flagship piece of content and link it from every service page
  5. Add structured project case studies (before/after, problem/solution/cost) to the galleries to deepen E-E-A-T signals
