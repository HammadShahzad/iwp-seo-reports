1 Executive Summary
64/100
Needs Work

Hoke Ley has a real foundation to build on: an award-winning, multi-disciplinary practice across architecture, interior design, equestrian, and environmental branding, with offices in Lawrence, Kansas City, and Scottsdale. The site looks beautiful and the project imagery is striking. From an SEO standpoint, though, the site is leaving a lot of qualified Kansas City and Lawrence search traffic on the table — search engines see a small slice of what the firm actually does, and the firm is competing against larger Kansas City studios (Hufft, Generator Studio) that are doing more structured SEO work.

The biggest issues are the H1 on the homepage (it's just "Hoke Ley" — no keywords, no city, no specialty), 19 of 31 homepage images missing alt text (a serious problem for a portfolio-driven firm where images are the product), and no LocalBusiness schema on either the Kansas City or Lawrence location. With a focused 4-6 week effort across on-page fixes, schema, and a few service-area pages, the site can climb meaningfully for "architect Kansas City" and "residential architect Lawrence KS" — both of which currently feature directory sites and bigger firms above Hoke Ley.
