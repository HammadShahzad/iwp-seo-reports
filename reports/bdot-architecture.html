1 Executive Summary
56/100
Critical

bDot Architecture has a striking, award-recognized portfolio and a clean, well-designed website -- but much of that strength is invisible to search engines. The site scores 56 out of 100. The homepage is reasonably well optimized with a location-aware title and description, yet the portfolio pages that showcase your best work are built as bare image galleries with no headings, no descriptions, and almost no image alt text.

The biggest opportunity is straightforward: make your projects findable. Right now someone searching for a 'residential architect in Birmingham' or a 'modern farmhouse architect' is unlikely to land on your work, because those pages lack the text, structure, and individual project pages that search engines reward. Competitors like Studio C and CCR publish far more written content and dedicated service pages. A focused round of on-page fixes, plus individual project pages, would let bDot compete on visibility the way it already competes on design.

6 Content Gap Recommendations

Topics and content types your competitors cover that you're missing.

Individual project case studies High Priority

Each project page can rank for project-type and style searches while showing prospective clients how you work.

Recommended format: Individual project pages with photos, project scope, and a short narrative

Service pages for each practice area High Priority

Searches like 'residential architect Birmingham' and 'commercial architect Birmingham' need dedicated, text-rich pages to rank.

Recommended format: One landing page per service, written around local keywords

Image alt text across the site High Priority

Alt text is how Google 'sees' architectural photography -- without it your strongest work is invisible to image search.

Recommended format: Descriptive alt text on every project image

Cost and process explainer content Medium Priority

Informational searches such as 'how much does an architect cost' reach clients early in their decision.

Recommended format: Blog posts or a dedicated FAQ page

Sustainability / LEED page Medium Priority

Your director is LEED AP -- a dedicated page captures green-building searches and builds trust.

Recommended format: A page describing your sustainable design approach and credentials

7 Prioritized Action Plan
Quick Wins do this week — under 2 hours each
  1. Add unique meta descriptions to the Houses, Office & Health, and Retail & Restaurants pages
  2. Write descriptive alt text for every image on the portfolio pages, starting with the homepage and Houses
