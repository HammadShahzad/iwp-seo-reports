1 Executive Summary
48/100
Critical

Capaldo Builders has a strong story to tell — custom home building, design-build expertise, green building practices, and a long-running gallery of completed projects across Central Ohio. The trust signals are there: licensed, bonded, insured, Energy Star Partner, Ohio Farm Bureau memberships, and an active presence on Houzz, Facebook, Pinterest, and Instagram. That is a real foundation to build on.

The website itself is holding the brand back. The platform is an aging WordPress theme (circa 2010), title tags are heavily keyword-stuffed, meta descriptions are duplicated across pages, image filenames and alt text don't help search engines understand the work, and there's no LocalBusiness or GeneralContractor schema to support local search. The mixed http/https image references and outdated tracking scripts also hurt trust signals. The good news is most of these issues are straightforward to fix and would meaningfully improve how Capaldo Builders shows up when Columbus homeowners search for a custom home builder or remodeler.

6 Content Gap Recommendations

Topics and content types your competitors cover that you're missing.

Service area pages for Dublin, Upper Arlington, Bexley, New Albany, Westerville, Powell High Priority

Each suburb is a distinct search term with real volume — competitors are ranking on these and Capaldo isn't. Existing project work in Dublin already gives strong content to anchor a page on.

Recommended format: One page per suburb, ~600 words each, with a project example, service list, and a localized FAQ

Cost guide: 'How much does it cost to build a custom home in Columbus?' High Priority

This is the single highest-volume informational query for the segment. It captures researching homeowners 6-12 months before they hire — they read it, then come back when they're ready.

Recommended format: Long-form article (1,500+ words) with cost ranges by home size, factors that affect price, and a CTA to request an estimate

Project case studies (replacing or expanding the gallery descriptions) High Priority

Each gallery page currently shows photos but very little written context. Adding 200-300 words per project (scope, challenge, solution, timeline, materials used) gives Google something to rank on AND helps prospects understand the work.

Recommended format: Detailed case study page per project with embedded gallery, client quote if available, and project specs

Process / 'What to expect when you build with us' Medium Priority

First-time custom home buyers are nervous and search for what the process looks like. A clear page reduces friction and ranks for 'custom home building process'.

Recommended format: Step-by-step page with a visual timeline and downloadable PDF guide

Sustainable / green building landing page Medium Priority

Capaldo already does green work but it's buried in a paragraph on the services page. Eco-conscious Columbus homeowners are an underserved niche worth owning.

Recommended format: Dedicated page covering geothermal, energy-efficient materials, and Energy Star Partner status with project examples

Kitchen and bathroom remodel landing pages with before/after galleries Medium Priority

Remodeling is mentioned in passing but not given its own page. These are high-intent searches that send qualified leads.

Recommended format: Two separate pages — Kitchen Remodeling and Bathroom Renovation — each with project gallery, scope, typical timeline, and FAQ

7 Prioritized Action Plan
Quick Wins do this week — under 2 hours each
  1. Rewrite the homepage title tag and meta description to remove the keyword stuffing — make it sound human
  2. Add unique meta descriptions to the Services, Gallery, About, and Contact pages
  3. Add a viewport meta tag (<meta name=viewport content='width=device-width, initial-scale=1'>) to the theme header
  4. Replace the empty logo alt attribute with 'Capaldo Builders — Columbus Home Builder'
  5. Update all internal image references from http:// to https:// to clear mixed-content warnings
  6. Add Open Graph tags (og:title, og:description, og:image) so links shared on Facebook, LinkedIn, and iMessage show a real preview
  7. Migrate Google Analytics from the legacy ga.js to GA4 — current setup stopped recording data
  8. Drop the meta keywords tag — it does nothing and looks dated to anyone auditing the site
  9. Set the duplicate H1 on the Services page to a single H1 and convert the rest to H2
  10. Submit an XML sitemap to Google Search Console
Strategic Investments plan for this quarter
  1. Migrate to a modern, mobile-first WordPress theme (or rebuild on a cleaner platform) — single biggest lift to mobile experience and Core Web Vitals
  2. Add LocalBusiness / GeneralContractor JSON-LD schema with full NAP, hours, service areas, and aggregateRating from Houzz/Google reviews
  3. Build out 6 service area pages (Dublin, Upper Arlington, Bexley, New Albany, Westerville, Powell) — each anchored to a real Capaldo project nearby
  4. Replace the current gallery with full case study pages — 200-300 words of context per project plus optimized image filenames and alt text
  5. Publish a flagship cost guide and a 'how to choose a builder' guide to capture top-of-funnel informational searches
  6. Stand up two dedicated remodeling pages (kitchen, bathroom) with before/after content and structured data
  7. Launch a quarterly blog cadence — even 4 strong posts per year, indexed properly, beats an inactive blog category
  8. Set up review collection on Google Business Profile and embed reviews with schema markup on the testimonials page
