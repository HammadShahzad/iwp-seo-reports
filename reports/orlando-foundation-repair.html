1 Executive Summary
54/100
Critical

Orlando Foundation Repair has a genuinely strong story to tell — 8,000+ projects completed since 1989, a zero failure record, lifetime warranties, financing through Greensky and Enerbank, BBB membership. The problem is none of that story shows up on Google the way it should. The site is a single long page of mostly unbroken text, the homepage title is just 'Orlando Foundation Repair,' and there are no individual service pages for the things homeowners actually search for in Central Florida — sinkhole stabilization, helical piers, slab repair, seawall, crawl space encapsulation. When someone in Winter Park types 'foundation repair near me' or 'house settling Orlando,' there's almost nothing on the site for Google to match them to.

Three things are holding back results: a thin title and missing meta description on the homepage, zero dedicated service pages for the eight services listed in the body copy, and no local trust signals (LocalBusiness schema, NAP block, embedded map). Big competitors like Solid Foundations and LRE Foundation Repair are out-ranking you on the searches that bring homeowners with real money to spend — not because their work is better, but because their site architecture matches how people search. The good news: most of the fixes are content reorganization, not new product development. A focused 60 days of work could realistically move several key Orlando service-area searches onto page one.

6 Content Gap Recommendations

Topics and content types your competitors cover that you're missing.

Signs your Florida home has foundation problems High Priority

Highest-volume informational query in this niche — homeowners spot a wall crack and Google their way to a contractor. Owning this query feeds the entire funnel.

Recommended format: Pillar blog post with diagnostic photos, FAQ schema, and inline CTA to free inspection

Sinkhole stabilization in Central Florida — what to expect High Priority

Sinkholes are a uniquely Florida concern and a high-ticket service. Your lifetime sinkhole warranty is a real differentiator that nobody can see on Google right now.

Recommended format: Dedicated service page + process walkthrough with timeline, cost range, and insurance notes

Helical piers vs push piers — which does my house need? High Priority

Comparison queries convert because the homeowner is already past the 'is this a problem' stage. Currently zero of your pier expertise is indexed.

Recommended format: Comparison article with diagrams and a 'what we recommend when' table

Foundation repair cost in Orlando — 2026 guide High Priority

Cost is the #1 informational query before any contractor decision. Your competitors own these queries.

Recommended format: Cost guide with ranges by service, financing call-out, free-inspection CTA

Crawl space encapsulation for Florida homes Medium Priority

Florida humidity drives strong search demand. You mention crawl space repair but have no dedicated page.

Recommended format: Service page with before/after, humidity science, warranty mention

Service area pages — Winter Park, Kissimmee, Lake Mary, Sanford, Apopka, Oviedo High Priority

Hyper-local pages capture 'foundation repair [neighborhood]' searches that homeowners actually use. Your 8,000-project history makes you credible everywhere.

Recommended format: One page per sub-market with location-specific copy, embedded map, local testimonial

How the 25-year product warranty + lifetime sinkhole warranty work Medium Priority

Your warranty is genuinely strong but buried in body text. A dedicated page would turn it into a closing argument.

Recommended format: Trust page with side-by-side warranty comparison and FAQ

Project case studies and before/after gallery Medium Priority

8,000+ projects since 1989 is your single strongest proof point and currently invisible. Case studies also rank well for long-tail searches.

Recommended format: Filterable gallery with 15-20 projects, each with problem/solution/photo

7 Prioritized Action Plan
Quick Wins do this week — under 2 hours each
  1. Rewrite the homepage title tag to include 'Orlando, FL' and a key service — 'Foundation Repair Orlando, FL | Sinkholes, Helical Piers, Slab Lifting Since 1989'
  2. Add a 150-160 character meta description leading with 'family-owned since 1989' and 'free inspection'
  3. Remove the keyword-stuffed paragraph in the middle of the homepage — it reads as spam
  4. Add a consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) block in the site footer on every page
  5. Add LocalBusiness schema with hours, address, phone, service area, and review aggregate
  6. Add descriptive alt text to every image using service + location wording
  7. Claim and fully fill out Google Business Profile if not already (categories, services, photos, Q&A)
  8. Break the homepage body into proper H2 sections (Services / Process / Warranty / Service Area / Reviews)
Strategic Investments plan for this quarter
  1. Build out 8 dedicated service pages — sinkhole stabilization, helical piers, push piers, slab repair, concrete lifting, crawl space encapsulation, seawall, foundation cracks — each 800-1,200 words with photos
  2. Build 6 service-area pages for Winter Park, Kissimmee, Lake Mary, Sanford, Apopka, and Oviedo with location-specific copy and embedded maps
  3. Launch a real blog with a content calendar — start with cost guide, warning signs guide, helical vs push pier comparison, sinkhole walkthrough
  4. Build a project gallery with 15-20 before/after case studies from the 8,000-project history (with permission)
  5. Add a dedicated warranty/trust page covering lifetime sinkhole stabilization warranty + 25-year product warranty
  6. Add Review + AggregateRating schema to surface star ratings in Google results
  7. Build a financing page covering Greensky and Enerbank options — this is a closing argument that competitors highlight
  8. Implement a structured internal linking plan so the homepage feeds traffic into service pages and service-area pages
