1 Executive Summary
58/100
Critical

Bonilla Foundation Repair has an enormous trust advantage that most local foundation companies would envy — 30+ years in Houston, a deep bench of detailed customer reviews, family ownership, and a lifetime transferable warranty. The site does a solid job of communicating that story, and the slab foundation services page reads well. The fundamentals are in place: HTTPS, mobile viewport, a meta description, a clear H1, and area pages for ten Houston suburbs.

Where the site is leaving money on the table is search visibility. Page titles are stuffed and not click-optimized, there are no individual pages for the specific repair methods Bonilla actually performs (pressed pilings, pier and beam underpinning, house leveling, structural repair), there is no LocalBusiness or Review schema markup so Google can't see the 30+ five-star reviews as structured data, and image alt text is largely decorative or missing. The Thryv site builder also bloats the page with duplicate content blocks. Fixing the on-page SEO and adding service-specific pages would meaningfully increase the number of Houston homeowners who find the site through organic search.

Strategic Investments plan for this quarter
