1 Executive Summary
61/100
Needs Work

Louis Cherry Architecture has built an impressive portfolio over the years — 26 individually indexable residential project pages, plus dedicated sections for restaurants, community, and interiors. That's already further than most boutique firms get, and it gives Google real depth to work with. The structural bones of the site are good, but the on-page SEO and image optimization haven't kept pace with the work itself.

The biggest wins are within reach: image alt text is largely missing or auto-generated (file names like '_A7R0509_R.jpg' as alt text), each project page is light on descriptive copy, and there's no LocalBusiness or Architect schema telling search engines you're a Raleigh practice. A focused round of metadata cleanup, alt text, and structured data could move you noticeably up the rankings for searches like 'modern residential architect Raleigh' and 'custom home architect Raleigh NC' — terms competitors are quietly capturing today.

6 Content Gap Recommendations

Topics and content types your competitors cover that you're missing.

Project case studies with process, materials, and outcome High Priority

Each of your 26 residential pages is a chance to rank for specific search terms like 'modern home architect Chapel Hill' or 'Raleigh ADU design' — but they need narrative copy to do that work

Recommended format: 200-400 word writeup per project page with H2 sections (concept, materials, site)

Service area pages for Chapel Hill, Durham, and the Triangle High Priority

You already have projects in these cities — dedicated pages capture 'architect [city]' searches with very low competition

Recommended format: Individual landing pages with 400-600 words + featured local projects

Sustainability / green design page Medium Priority

Your residential meta description mentions 'environmentally friendly homes' — but there's no page to rank for sustainability terms

Recommended format: Dedicated page explaining your approach, certifications, and example projects

Educational blog (cost, process, FAQs) Medium Priority

Informational searches like 'how much does an architect cost in NC' or 'architect vs designer' attract people early in their journey and build long-term authority

Recommended format: Short blog posts (600-1000 words), 1 per month is enough

Adaptive reuse and historic renovation page Medium Priority

Your bio mentions adaptive reuse — this is a low-competition niche where you could own the Triangle market

Recommended format: Service page with 2-3 featured examples

ADU / accessory dwelling unit page High Priority

ADU searches are growing fast in Raleigh as zoning changes — you have a Poolside ADU project to lead with

Recommended format: Service-focused landing page + the existing project as a case study
