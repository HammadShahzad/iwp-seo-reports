1 Executive Summary
58/100
Critical

Hatch Design Architecture has a polished, image-forward website with clean branding and a clear specialty mix across commercial, retail, medical, residential, retirement, and industrial work. The fundamentals like HTTPS, mobile viewport, canonical tags, and a Yoast-generated sitemap are all in place — so search engines can crawl the site without obstacles.

The bigger opportunity is in how the site speaks to Google about what it does and where it does it. The homepage has no H1 heading, almost every project image lacks descriptive alt text, service pages are missing meta descriptions, and there is no LocalBusiness or Architect schema for Boise. Each project lives buried inside a category page rather than as its own indexable case study, which means searches like 'restaurant architect Boise' or 'medical office architect Idaho' have very little to rank on. Fixing these well-defined items would meaningfully improve how Hatch shows up for the searches that matter most.

6 Content Gap Recommendations

Topics and content types your competitors cover that you're missing.

Individual project case study pages High Priority

Each project name currently sits as an H2 inside a category page. Splitting them out gives Google something specific to rank for searches like 'modern coffee shop architect Boise' and showcases process, materials, and outcomes.

Recommended format: One page per project with 250-400 words, hero photo, before/after, and a short interview-style writeup

Service-area pages for surrounding cities High Priority

Hatch already serves the broader Treasure Valley. Pages for Meridian, Eagle, Nampa, and Caldwell would capture local searches that the Boise homepage cannot reach on its own.

Recommended format: Short, original pages (300-500 words) with local references, completed nearby projects, and a Boise contact CTA

Tenant improvement and franchise rollout guide High Priority

These are explicit subject options on the contact form, suggesting a real client mix, but neither has its own page. Both have low-competition long-tail demand.

Recommended format: Two dedicated services pages with FAQs and a sample project

Cost / process explainer content Medium Priority

Phrases like 'how much does an architect cost in Idaho' and 'when do I need an architect' have steady search demand and are early-funnel for inquiries.

Recommended format: 2-3 educational blog posts with internal links to relevant services

Sustainability / energy code expertise Medium Priority

Idaho's commercial energy code and accessibility requirements are common questions. A page demonstrating expertise here builds trust with developers and franchisees.

Recommended format: One pillar page with subtopics on accessibility, energy code, and ADA
