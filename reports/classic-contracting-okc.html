1 Executive Summary
54/100
Critical

Your site does a solid job of covering the full range of services you offer — from new home construction to roofing, concrete, and kitchen and bath remodels — and it loads quickly with a clear structure. That foundation is genuinely useful, and a lot of contractors don't have a service breakdown this organized. The challenge is that almost none of that work is currently showing up where Oklahoma City homeowners are searching. The homepage title doesn't mention Oklahoma City, the contact email runs through Gmail instead of your own domain, your testimonials section reads 'Coming Soon,' and there's no project gallery or schema markup helping Google understand who you serve.

The good news is these are fixable issues, not architectural problems. With targeted on-page work — a real LocalBusiness schema block, city-specific titles and meta descriptions, a portfolio with project photos and short case studies, and Google reviews surfaced on the site — you'd close most of the gap against SH Renovations and All About Remodeling within a quarter. You don't need a rebuild; you need the existing pages to start working harder for search.

6 Content Gap Recommendations

Topics and content types your competitors cover that you're missing.

Project portfolio with real photos and case studies High Priority

Homeowners in Oklahoma City want to see what you've actually built before they call. Right now your site shows stock construction images, which any contractor could use — there's nothing that proves the quality of your work or sets you apart from the dozens of other GCs in the metro.

Recommended format: Gallery page with 12-20 projects, each with 4-6 photos, project type, timeline, and a 2-3 sentence story

Customer reviews and testimonials High Priority

The 'Coming Soon' placeholders are actively hurting trust — visitors notice when a section looks unfinished. You already have a Google review link in the footer; the reviews exist, they're just not on the site where buyers can see them.

Recommended format: Reviews carousel pulled live from Google Business Profile, plus 3-4 full case-study testimonials per service

Service-area landing pages for OKC suburbs High Priority

Searches like 'home remodeling Edmond' or 'general contractor Norman' are easier to rank for than 'Oklahoma City' and often have buyers ready to hire. You serve these areas already — you just need pages that tell Google so.

Recommended format: Short, unique pages (300-500 words) for Edmond, Norman, Moore, Yukon, Mustang with local references and project examples

Cost and pricing guides Medium Priority

Homeowners search 'cost to remodel kitchen Oklahoma City' and 'price to add a room in OKC' constantly. Whoever ranks for these pulls in qualified leads weeks before they're ready to call. Right now those visitors land on competitors' blogs or HomeAdvisor.

FAQ sections on each service page with schema markup Medium Priority

Your service pages already touch on what each service involves — adding a structured FAQ at the bottom (with FAQ schema) gives you a shot at the 'People also ask' boxes in Google for high-intent queries.

Recommended format: 5-7 questions per service page, marked up with JSON-LD FAQPage schema

Educational blog content Medium Priority

Competitors publish guides like 'how to choose a contractor in Oklahoma' and 'what to expect during a remodel' that build authority and pull in informational traffic that converts later. You currently have nothing of the kind.

Recommended format: 1-2 posts per month, 800-1,200 words, focused on local homeowner questions
