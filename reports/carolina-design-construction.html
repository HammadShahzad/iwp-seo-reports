1 Executive Summary
58/100
Critical

Carolina Design & Construction has a solid foundation as a commercial general contractor serving Raleigh and the Triangle, with a strong portfolio, multiple industry service pages, and visible client testimonials. However, several technical SEO basics are missing — no meta descriptions on key pages, no LocalBusiness or GeneralContractor schema markup, and the vast majority of project images carry empty alt attributes — which means search engines can't fully understand or rank your project work. The keyword-stuffed homepage title also limits click-through from search results.

The biggest opportunity is local SEO and structured data: adding LocalBusiness schema, Cary/Durham/Apex service-area pages, and proper image descriptions to every portfolio project. Competitors like Barker Construction Group are ranking in part because they have cleaner page structure and a longer-established Raleigh presence; closing the on-page and schema gap should produce visible movement on commercial construction queries within 60–90 days.

6 Content Gap Recommendations

Topics and content types your competitors cover that you're missing.

Cary / Durham / Apex service-area pages High Priority

Commercial buyers search by city — separate pages capture geo-specific traffic and rank for 'commercial contractor [city]'

Recommended format: Dedicated pages, 600+ words, with embedded map and local project examples

Case studies for each portfolio project High Priority

A photo gallery doesn't rank — search engines and prospects need scope, timeline, square footage, and outcome to evaluate trust

Recommended format: Project pages with cover image, 300–500 word narrative, before/after photos, client quote

Tenant improvement / office build-out service page High Priority

TI is one of the highest-intent commercial GC searches in Raleigh and you don't currently target it

Recommended format: Single landing page with process, timeline, sample projects, FAQ

Cost guide blog posts Medium Priority

Cost-focused informational queries pull early-stage commercial buyers before they short-list contractors

Recommended format: Long-form blog post (1,500+ words) with cost ranges, factors, and an FAQ schema block

Industry-specific deep dives (medical, restaurant, retail) Medium Priority

You list industries served but each deserves its own page to rank for 'medical office contractor Raleigh', 'restaurant build out Raleigh' etc.

Recommended format: One landing page per industry with relevant projects and code/regulation considerations

FAQ section with FAQ schema Medium Priority

FAQ schema captures featured snippets and 'People also ask' boxes for high-intent queries

Recommended format: Site-wide FAQ block at bottom of services pages, marked up with FAQPage JSON-LD

7 Prioritized Action Plan
Quick Wins do this week — under 2 hours each
  1. Add a unique 150-character meta description to the homepage and every services/industries page
  2. Replace the keyword-stuffed homepage title with 'Commercial General Contractor in Raleigh, NC | Carolina Design & Construction'
  3. Update the homepage H1 to include a city + service keyword (e.g. 'Commercial General Contractor in Raleigh & The Triangle')
  4. Audit every image on homepage and portfolio — write descriptive alt text including project type, client name, and city where appropriate
  5. Add Open Graph (og:title, og:description, og:image) and Twitter card meta tags so shared links preview cleanly
  6. Make sure your Google Business Profile is fully filled out with photos, services, hours, and a request-a-quote CTA
  7. Verify your name, address, and phone number match exactly across the site footer, GBP, and major directories
Strategic Investments plan for this quarter
  1. Add LocalBusiness + GeneralContractor JSON-LD schema sitewide, plus Service schema on each industry/service page
  2. Build dedicated service-area pages for Cary, Durham, Apex, Wake Forest, and Chapel Hill — 600+ words each, with local project examples
  3. Convert the project portfolio from a photo gallery into individual case-study pages (scope, timeline, square footage, client outcome, photos)
  4. Create a tenant improvement / office build-out landing page targeting 'office build out Raleigh' and 'tenant improvement contractor Raleigh'
  5. Launch a content calendar with 1–2 blog posts per month focused on cost guides, project process, and industry-specific topics (medical, restaurant, retail buildouts)
  6. Migrate hero and portfolio images to WebP and lazy-load below-the-fold media to improve Core Web Vitals
