1 Executive Summary
56/100
Critical

Shamrock Waterproofing has a strong local story — over 30 years serving the Triangle, hands-on owner involvement, and a long list of detailed five-star reviews that competitors would love to have. The site reflects the business honestly, with a clear service menu and real testimonials, but it's punching well below its weight in search. Page load is slow (8+ seconds), the homepage has 5 separate H1 tags instead of one, and the meta description reads more like a paragraph than a search snippet.

The biggest missed opportunity is local SEO foundation work. There's no LocalBusiness schema telling Google this is a Raleigh waterproofing company, no individual city pages for Durham, Cary, Chapel Hill, or Apex, and zero educational content (blog or FAQ) about the questions homeowners actually search — wet basements, crawl space costs, signs of foundation issues. A focused 4–6 week effort on the items below would meaningfully change how often Shamrock shows up when a Triangle homeowner has water in their basement at 9pm on a Tuesday.

6 Content Gap Recommendations

Topics and content types your competitors cover that you're missing.

Signs of Foundation Problems in NC Homes High Priority

High-volume informational query that turns into commercial intent once a homeowner sees their wall crack matches a photo. Brings trust-building organic traffic before competitors get the click.

Recommended format: Long-form blog post with diagnostic photos, FAQ schema, and links to your foundation repair service page

How Much Does Basement Waterproofing Cost in Raleigh? High Priority

Homeowners search cost questions constantly. Most competitors avoid publishing ranges — being transparent here builds trust and ranks easily.

Recommended format: Article with cost range tables (interior vs exterior, sump add-ons, etc.) and a 'Get a Free Estimate' CTA

Crawl Space Encapsulation: What's Included and Why It Matters High Priority

Crawl space is one of your best margins and Triangle homes have a high percentage of crawl space foundations. Big informational keyword opportunity.

Recommended format: Pillar page with diagrams, process steps, before/after photos, FAQ

Service Area Pages for Durham, Cary, Chapel Hill, Apex High Priority

Right now the homepage tries to rank everywhere. Dedicated city pages with localized content win 'service + city' searches that competitors are dominating.

Recommended format: One 600–800 word page per city with local references, reviews from that city, and service-specific content

Before & After Project Gallery Medium Priority

Image search and trust. Project photos rank in Google Images and convert hesitant homeowners on the fence between three quotes.

Recommended format: Filterable gallery (Basement / Crawl Space / Foundation / Drainage) with short captions describing the problem and solution

What to Do If You Find Water in Your Basement Medium Priority

Captures urgency searches at 9pm after a storm. Drives phone calls, not just form fills.

Recommended format: Action-oriented blog post with checklist, photos, and 'When to call us' section
