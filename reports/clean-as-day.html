1 Executive Summary
58/100
Critical

Clean As Day has a clean, professional homepage and the basics are in place — HTTPS, mobile viewport, a single H1, decent title tag, and an existing presence in a competitive local market. The brand voice is friendly and the service descriptions read well for actual humans. From a search-visibility standpoint, however, the site is operating with one hand tied behind its back: every service lives on the homepage as an anchor link, which means there are no dedicated URLs ranking for searches like "carpet cleaning Phoenix," "pet urine removal Phoenix," or "natural stone cleaning Scottsdale."

The biggest opportunity here isn't a technical overhaul — it's giving Google more pages to work with. Adding service pages, neighborhood/city pages for the Phoenix metro (Scottsdale, Mesa, Tempe, Chandler, Glendale), and a small blog covering common questions like stain removal or pet odor would multiply your visibility substantially. A few quick fixes — alt text on photos, a proper LocalBusiness schema with NAP, and trimming a duplicate H2 — would land in under two hours and move several rankings.

7 Prioritized Action Plan
Quick Wins do this week — under 2 hours each
  1. Add alt text to the 5 homepage images that are missing it (use descriptive phrases like "Steam carpet cleaning in Phoenix home")
  2. Replace the broken JSON-LD block with a valid LocalBusiness schema including business name, full address, phone, service area, and hours
  3. Remove the duplicate "Specialized Floor Cleaning Services" H2
  4. Expand the meta description from 115 to ~155 characters and include service area + a soft CTA
  5. Confirm /sitemap.xml exists and submit it in Google Search Console
  6. Clean up the H1 whitespace so the headline renders cleanly on one logical line
  7. Add a clear NAP (Name / Address / Phone) block in the footer for local SEO consistency
Strategic Investments plan for this quarter
  1. Build dedicated service pages for carpet cleaning, tile and grout, area rugs, natural stone, and pet urine removal — five new indexable URLs
  2. Build city pages for Scottsdale, Mesa, Tempe, Chandler, and Glendale — each with local landmarks, neighborhoods, and customer stories
  3. Launch a simple blog and publish 6-8 posts in the first 90 days targeting informational keywords (stain removal guides, how often to clean, pet odor tips)
  4. Set up a reviews page with schema markup pulling from your Google Business Profile to earn star markup in search results
  5. Build a pricing / FAQ page with FAQ schema to capture "cost of carpet cleaning Phoenix" type queries
  6. Earn 5-10 local backlinks through Phoenix-area chambers of commerce, neighborhood blogs, and partnerships with property managers
