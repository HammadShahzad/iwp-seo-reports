1 Executive Summary
54/100
Critical

The Carpet Guy has a clean, well-photographed single-page Squarespace site that does a good job telling Mike's story and listing services. The brand voice feels personal and trustworthy, which is a real asset in a category dominated by big chains. However, the site is leaving significant local search visibility on the table because it's built as one long page rather than as separate, indexable pages for each service and service area.

The biggest opportunities are structural: build dedicated pages for each major service and for the surrounding cities Mike already serves (Waxhaw, Indian Trail, Matthews, Ballantyne), update the dated 2012-2016 copyright that signals neglect to both visitors and Google, and add the basic local business schema markup that helps Google connect this site to the Google Business Profile. With these in place, The Carpet Guy can start ranking for the long-tail searches that competitors like Pronto & Carpet are currently capturing.

6 Content Gap Recommendations

Topics and content types your competitors cover that you're missing.

Dedicated service pages (Carpet, Upholstery, Tile & Grout, Pet Stains, Oriental Rug, Hardwood Refinishing) High Priority

Each service should have its own URL so it can rank for its own keyword cluster. Right now everything competes on a single page.

Recommended format: One landing page per service, 600-900 words each, with FAQs

Service area pages for Waxhaw, Indian Trail, Matthews, Ballantyne, Weddington High Priority

Long-tail local searches like 'carpet cleaning waxhaw nc' have low competition and high commercial intent. Mike already services these areas.

Recommended format: One page per city with localized testimonials and service details

Pricing guide / what affects carpet cleaning cost Medium Priority

Cost-related searches drive a huge share of informational traffic in this category and convert well when paired with a free estimate CTA.

Recommended format: Blog post with example pricing ranges and FAQ block

Pet stain and odor removal guide Medium Priority

Pet owners are a high-intent segment. A how-to guide that covers the limits of DIY and points to professional cleaning earns trust and links.

Recommended format: Blog post with step-by-step + before/after photos

Before and after gallery with project descriptions Medium Priority

The current gallery has images but minimal context. Captioned project case studies are great for SEO and conversion.

Recommended format: Dedicated gallery page with short writeups

Reviews / testimonials page with embedded Google reviews Low Priority

Social proof on-site lifts conversion and helps reviews rank in search results.

Recommended format: Reviews page with schema-marked testimonials

7 Prioritized Action Plan
Quick Wins do this week — under 2 hours each
  1. Update the 2012-2016 copyright in the footer to the current year
  2. Add a clear H1 heading 'Carpet Cleaning in Charlotte, NC' at the top of the page
  3. Rewrite the homepage title tag to lead with 'Carpet Cleaning Charlotte NC'
  4. Expand the meta description to ~155 characters with a free-estimate hook
