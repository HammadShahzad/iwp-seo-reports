Needs Work

Pronto & Carpet LLC has strong fundamentals: 20+ years in business, IICRC certification, A+ BBB rating, and multiple Angie's List Super Service Awards. The site loads cleanly, is mobile-friendly, and uses HTTPS. However, the SEO foundation is dated and is leaving local search traffic on the table to competitors who have caught up over the past decade.

The biggest gaps are around local SEO structure (no LocalBusiness schema, no neighborhood landing pages for Matthews / Ballantyne / Weddington / Marvin), thin homepage copy with a generic H1, and a split-brand setup where the domain is prontoandcarpetllc.com but the email and Twitter handle reference a different domain. Fixing the top items in section 7 should drive measurable local search gains within 30-60 days.

6 Content Gap Recommendations

Topics and content types your competitors cover that you're missing.

Charlotte neighborhood / suburb service area pages High Priority

You serve Matthews, Ballantyne, Weddington, and Marvin but have no individual pages — competitors rank locally for these because they do

Recommended format: One short landing page per neighborhood (300-500 words) with localized testimonials

Transparent pricing guide for Charlotte carpet cleaning High Priority

'Carpet cleaning cost charlotte' is a low-competition search with strong commercial intent; you already have an online quote tool but no indexable pricing content

Recommended format: Long-form article with sample quotes, per-room and per-square-foot ranges

Pet odor and urine removal landing page High Priority

Pet odor is currently buried inside the main carpet cleaning page; high-intent searchers want a dedicated page

Recommended format: Standalone service page with before/after photos and FAQ schema

Carpet cleaning blog (cadence: 2 posts/month) Medium Priority

Zero blog presence; informational queries ('how often,' 'best stain remover,' 'how long to dry') go to competitors and aggregators

Recommended format: Helpful how-to posts, 800-1200 words, with internal links to services

Commercial / B2B carpet cleaning case studies Medium Priority

Commercial page is generic; B2B buyers want proof points, insurance info, and after-hours availability

Recommended format: 2-3 case studies + dedicated commercial landing page with COI mention
