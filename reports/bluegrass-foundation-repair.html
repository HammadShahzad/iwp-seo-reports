1 Executive Summary
64/100
Needs Work

Bluegrass Foundation Repair has a clean, professional WordPress/Elementor site with strong service coverage — dedicated pages for foundation repair, basement waterproofing, crawl space repair, concrete leveling, basement wall repair, and egress windows. The brand presents well, the site is HTTPS-secured with canonical tags and proper Open Graph markup, and the homepage tells a clear story for Kentucky homeowners. The active blog and visible reviews are real assets most local competitors lack.

Where the site is leaving money on the table is in three places: every page is missing a meta description (so Google writes its own snippet from random body text), the title tags don't include the city or buyer-intent keywords, and there's no LocalBusiness schema or FAQ schema — which means the site isn't competing for the rich-result slots that 'foundation repair Louisville' searches now show. The site also serves the entire state but only has one Service Area page; building dedicated pages for Louisville, Lexington, Bowling Green, etc. would unlock organic traffic from each market individually.

6 Content Gap Recommendations

Topics and content types your competitors cover that you're missing.

Foundation repair cost guide for Kentucky homeowners High Priority

Cost queries are among the highest-volume informational searches in this category and they bring buyer-ready traffic. Competitors with cost guides outrank Bluegrass for these terms.

Recommended format: Long-form pillar post with cost ranges by repair type, factors that affect cost, and FAQ schema

Signs of foundation problems (homeowner diagnostic guide) High Priority

Top-of-funnel content that homeowners search before they know they need a repair. Strong internal-link opportunity to push readers into service pages.

Recommended format: Visual blog post with 8-10 warning signs, photos, and a 'when to call a pro' decision tree

City-specific landing pages (Louisville, Lexington, Bowling Green, Paducah, Frankfort) High Priority

Single statewide service area page is bleeding rankings to competitors who have built out city pages. Each city page can capture its own local search demand.

Recommended format: Dedicated landing pages with local content, neighborhoods served, recent projects, and city-specific schema

Crawl space encapsulation deep-dive Medium Priority

Encapsulation is a higher-ticket service and a content gap competitors aren't fully owning. Good ROI per page.

Recommended format: Service page expansion with before/after gallery, process explanation, and pricing context

Foundation crack repair: what each crack type means Medium Priority

Highly searched diagnostic query. Bluegrass already has the expertise — just needs to publish it as content.

Recommended format: Visual guide with photos of vertical, horizontal, stair-step cracks and what each indicates

After-repair maintenance & warranty guide Low Priority

Trust-building content that supports the sales cycle and gives existing customers reasons to return to the site.

Recommended format: Owner's guide PDF download or long-form post with warranty details
