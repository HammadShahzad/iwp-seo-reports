1 Executive Summary
61/100
Needs Work

Apex Foundation Experts has a clear story — a locally owned, family-operated foundation specialist serving the Mid-South, with three brothers running the show. That positioning is gold in a market where most competitors are franchises or corporate dealers. The site looks clean and modern, the photography is honest, and the 'A Family Helping Families' message lands. The bones are solid.

The trouble is that Google and Memphis homeowners can't easily find that story. Critical on-page elements are incomplete (the homepage H1 is cut off mid-sentence), there's no LocalBusiness schema telling search engines who and where you are, and there are no dedicated city-specific pages for the suburbs you serve (Bartlett, Germantown, Collierville, Cordova). Add some informational blog content around the questions homeowners actually search — 'signs of foundation problems', 'foundation repair cost Memphis', 'cracks in walls' — and you'd start showing up well before the franchise dealers.

6 Content Gap Recommendations

Topics and content types your competitors cover that you're missing.

Signs of foundation problems in Memphis homes High Priority

Top-of-funnel search query that drives organic traffic and builds trust before homeowners even know who to call. High volume in your market

Recommended format: Long-form guide (1,500+ words) with photos of each warning sign + FAQ schema

Foundation repair cost in Memphis — what to expect High Priority

Cost is the #1 question on every homeowner's mind. Owning this query positions Apex as the transparent local choice and pre-qualifies leads

Recommended format: Pricing guide with ranges by repair type (piers, waterproofing, drainage), factors affecting cost, financing notes

7 Prioritized Action Plan
Quick Wins do this week — under 2 hours each
  1. Fix the homepage H1 — it currently ends mid-sentence with 'in' and no location
  2. Rewrite the homepage meta description to lead with 'Memphis foundation repair' and include a phone number
  3. Add LocalBusiness JSON-LD schema with NAP, hours, service areas, and aggregateRating
  4. Add FAQPage schema to any existing FAQ content on the homepage or services page
  5. Submit (or re-submit) your XML sitemap to Google Search Console and confirm indexing
  6. Add internal links from the homepage to each major service section using descriptive anchor text
  7. Optimize image filenames before upload (e.g., 'helical-pier-installation-memphis.jpg' not 'IMG_4571.jpg')
  8. Surface your Memphis Most / awards / BBB rating prominently above the fold for trust signals
Strategic Investments plan for this quarter
  1. Split the aggregated services page into 5-7 dedicated service URLs, each 800+ words with project photos and FAQs
  2. Build dedicated suburb landing pages for Bartlett, Germantown, Collierville, Cordova, and Olive Branch MS — at least 600 words each
  3. Publish a content cluster of 8-12 cornerstone informational articles answering the questions homeowners actually search
  4. Build out a real before/after project gallery with descriptions, location (suburb), and the specific repair method used
  5. Add video content — even a 60-second walkthrough by one of the brothers builds enormous trust and improves dwell time
  6. Earn 5-10 local citations and links from Memphis-area home services blogs, real estate agents, and home inspector networks
  7. Set up Google Search Console + GA4 properly and review monthly so you know which queries are converting
