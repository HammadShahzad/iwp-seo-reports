1 Executive Summary
54/100
Critical

Cream City Construction has a fantastic story to tell — 50+ years in business, a beautiful office in the historic Pabst coach house, and a portfolio that clearly competes at the high end of Milwaukee design-build. The trouble is that almost none of that comes through in search. The site is built on an aging Weebly template, has no dedicated service pages for kitchens, bathrooms, additions, or whole-house remodels, and the gallery — your strongest asset — is essentially invisible to Google because images sit on /uploads/ paths with no descriptive alt text or structured data.

On the technical side, the basics are mostly fine (HTTPS, mobile viewport, decent meta tags), but there is no LocalBusiness or GeneralContractor schema, no city/neighborhood landing pages (Whitefish Bay, Wauwatosa, Shorewood — all mentioned in your keywords meta tag but with zero supporting pages), and there are visible typos in page titles (e.g. 'design and building process for Cram City Construction'). The good news: most of these are quick fixes, and a focused 60-day push on service pages and local schema would meaningfully move the needle on phrases like 'Milwaukee home remodel' and 'historic home renovation Milwaukee.'

6 Content Gap Recommendations

Topics and content types your competitors cover that you're missing.

Individual service pages (Kitchens, Bathrooms, Additions, Lower Levels, Master Suites, Whole-House) High Priority

These are the exact search phrases Milwaukee homeowners use, and you currently have nothing dedicated. Competitors rank above you because of this alone.

Recommended format: Six 700–1000 word pages, each with a hero image, 3–4 portfolio shots, FAQs, process snapshot, and CTA

Neighborhood landing pages — Whitefish Bay, Wauwatosa, Shorewood, Brookfield, Mequon High Priority

You serve these markets and even list them in your meta keywords. Unique pages signal local relevance and rank fast in suburb-specific searches.

Recommended format: 400–600 word pages each with a local project example and an embedded map

Historic home renovation in Milwaukee High Priority

You operate out of the Pabst coach house — almost no other Milwaukee remodeler can credibly own this niche. Big differentiator for high-end clients.

Recommended format: Dedicated page plus 2–3 blog posts on Cream City brick, plaster repair, etc.

Cost & budget guides (kitchen, bathroom, addition) Medium Priority

Cost is the #1 informational search homeowners run before contacting a remodeler. Owning these pages captures top-of-funnel traffic.

Recommended format: Blog-style guides with real Milwaukee-area budget ranges

Project case studies with full storyline Medium Priority

Your gallery shows the finished result but tells no story. Case studies build trust and naturally rank for long-tail searches.

Recommended format: Long-form case studies, 600–800 words each, with timeline, challenge, solution, and result

Testimonials with Review schema Medium Priority

Right now reviews are plain text. Marked up properly, Google can show star ratings in search results.

Recommended format: Restructure existing testimonials page with JSON-LD Review markup
