1 Executive Summary
66/100
Needs Work

Steven Ginn Architects has a genuinely beautiful, modern website that reflects the firm's design-forward, boutique sensibility — full-screen video, a strong portfolio, and thoughtful storytelling on each project. The foundation is solid: the site is on HTTPS, mobile-ready, project pages are individually indexable, and most pages carry custom meta descriptions. That puts the firm ahead of many small residential architects.

The biggest gaps are in the details that help Google understand the site. The homepage title and H1 lead with a brand tagline rather than the words people actually search — like 'residential architect Omaha' — so the site is leaving local search traffic on the table. The portfolio leans heavily on imagery and video that search engines can't 'read' without descriptive alt text, and there's no LocalBusiness/Architect schema telling Google the firm's name, address, and service area. These are mostly quick, high-leverage fixes rather than a rebuild.
