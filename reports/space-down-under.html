1 Executive Summary
38/100
Critical

Space Down Under has a genuinely unique niche — tight-access foundation replacement, house lifting, and basement ADU conversions in Portland's older neighborhoods. The craftsmanship in the project galleries is impressive and the company has decades of trade experience. The problem is that almost none of that comes through when a Portland homeowner searches Google for foundation help. The site is built on the older GoDaddy Website Builder, the homepage title is just the company name, the footer still says Copyright 2020, and there are no individual service pages for the work the company actually does.

The single biggest opportunity here is structure. Right now everything lives on one Services page with three-word captions like 'Please call for more information.' Splitting that into real pages — Foundation Replacement Portland, House Lifting Portland, Basement ADU Conversion Portland, Tight Access Excavation, Helical Pier Installation — would unlock most of the missed traffic. Add a proper meta description, fix the 'Testimonals' typo in the nav, refresh the copyright date, and the site immediately starts looking active again to both Google and homeowners. The foundation (no pun intended) is there; it just needs to be excavated and reframed.
