1 Executive Summary
61/100
Needs Work

Metro Foundation Solutions has a solid base — a clean site structure, separate service pages for foundation repair, waterproofing, crawl space, drainage, and gutters, plus an active testimonials section and BBB A+ rating. The site is HTTPS-secured, mobile-responsive, and the local address in Orange Park is clearly displayed. Most of the building blocks for ranking well in Jacksonville and Northeast Florida are already in place.

The biggest gaps are missed local SEO opportunities and content depth. Several service pages reuse the same generic copy and the meta descriptions reference Daytona Beach even when the page targets Jacksonville. There are no dedicated city pages for St. Augustine, Ocala, Palm Coast, or the Jacksonville neighborhoods you serve, no LocalBusiness schema, and the blog is thin on the informational content homeowners actually search for (signs of foundation issues, repair costs, pier installation explained). Closing these gaps would meaningfully increase organic leads from homeowners researching foundation problems.
