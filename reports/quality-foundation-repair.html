1 Executive Summary
64/100
Needs Work

Quality Foundation Repair has built a strong reputation in Omaha with 30+ years of experience, A+ BBB accreditation, and a deep portfolio of homeowner reviews. That trust signal is one of the strongest things going for the site. The pages cover the right service categories — waterproofing, structural repair, repointing, crawl space, egress, and dehumidification — and each one lives on its own URL, which is a good base to build from.

Where the site is leaving search traffic on the table is in the title tags, meta descriptions, and the lack of city-targeted landing pages. The homepage title repeats “QFR” twice with no city or service keyword, the meta descriptions read like body copy rather than search snippets, and there’s no dedicated content for Lincoln, Council Bluffs, Bellevue, Papillion, or the other communities listed in the service area. The address shown in the header (4350 S 90th St) doesn’t match the address on the contact page (1617 S 44th St), which can confuse Google about NAP consistency. Tightening those fundamentals plus adding a few cornerstone informational guides should move the needle quickly.
