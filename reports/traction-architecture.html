1 Executive Summary
68/100
Needs Work

Traction Architecture has built an impressive reputation — Forbes Top 200 Residential Architects, AIA Tampa Bay Firm Award, and press in Dwell, Architectural Digest, and the Wall Street Journal. The website does a beautiful job showcasing that work visually, and the foundation is solid: clean meta description, LocalBusiness schema, mobile-responsive layout, and dedicated project pages for each home.

Where there's real room to grow is in helping search engines actually surface this work. Page load is on the slow side (7.6s to fully load on the homepage), project pages are missing location keywords and structured descriptions that Google rewards, and a handful of images still lack alt text — meaning the visual story isn't being told to search crawlers. With targeted on-page work, this site could rank dramatically higher for searches like 'modern residential architect Tampa' and 'coastal architect Florida.'
