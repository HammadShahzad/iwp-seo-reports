1 Executive Summary
58/100
Critical

C. Thomas Cleaning Service has a real foundation to work with — 40+ years in business, an active Google Business Profile, social proof from customer reviews, and a clean Wix-built site that loads on HTTPS. The basics like a meta description, an H1, and a working contact form are all in place, which is more than many local competitors can say.

The site is, however, leaving a lot of ranking opportunity on the table. The title tags and meta descriptions are bloated and keyword-stuffed (which Google actively discounts now), the homepage content is heavily repeated and reads like filler, there's no LocalBusiness schema, image alt text reads like a keyword list, and the copyright still says 2025. Most importantly, there are no dedicated landing pages for the surrounding cities you serve (Midlothian, Mechanicsville, Glen Allen, Short Pump, Chesterfield) — which is where most local search traffic actually lives.

6 Content Gap Recommendations

Topics and content types your competitors cover that you're missing.

City / service-area landing pages (Midlothian, Mechanicsville, Glen Allen, Short Pump, Chesterfield) High Priority

This is where ~60% of local search traffic actually ranks. Competitors who built these pages outrank you on neighborhood-specific searches

Recommended format: One landing page per city (300-500 words each, unique content, local photos if possible)

Pet stain and odor removal High Priority

High-intent search with lower competition — pet owners are a large slice of carpet cleaning demand

Recommended format: Dedicated service page + supporting blog post

Transparent pricing / what to expect High Priority

Most local competitors hide pricing — being even partly transparent builds trust and converts better

Recommended format: Pricing page with starting ranges by service + room count

Commercial / office carpet cleaning Medium Priority

B2B carpet jobs are larger tickets and recurring revenue. Your site barely mentions commercial work

Recommended format: Dedicated Commercial Services page + 1-2 case studies

Method explainers (steam vs dry, eco-friendly) Medium Priority

Informational searches that build authority and feed your service pages

Recommended format: Blog posts with FAQ schema

Before & after photo gallery Medium Priority

Visual proof is one of the strongest conversion drivers in this industry — and helps with image search

Recommended format: Gallery page with descriptive captions and alt text
