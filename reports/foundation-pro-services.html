1 Executive Summary

Needs Work

Foundation Pro Services has a clean, professional site with solid trust signals (Google Guaranteed, BBB A+, Angi Super Service) and a real story behind it — Jim Brooks has lived in Birmingham over 60 years. The site has dedicated service pages for foundation repair, basement waterproofing, crawl space encapsulation, sump pumps, concrete leveling, and exterior water management, which is a genuine strength most local competitors lack.

That said, there are a few specific things holding back search visibility. The homepage is missing an H1 tag entirely, the contact email is a Gmail address (a small but real trust signal for homeowners), there is no LocalBusiness schema markup, and the blog is empty — meaning the site captures almost no informational traffic from homeowners researching foundation problems. Fixing these gaps would lift the site from a transactional-only presence to a full local-search authority.

6 Content Gap Recommendations

Topics and content types your competitors cover that you're missing.

Signs your home needs foundation repair (with photos) High Priority

Highest-volume informational query in this space — homeowners notice cracks and Google before calling. Currently zero presence in this topic.

Recommended format: Pillar blog post with embedded FAQ schema, 1500+ words, real photos from past jobs

Foundation repair cost guide for Alabama homeowners High Priority

Cost is the #1 question before booking. Owning this query funnels qualified leads to the contact form.

Recommended format: Cost guide page with price ranges by repair type, factors that affect cost, financing options

Alabama clay soil and your foundation — what every Birmingham homeowner should know High Priority

Hyper-local pain point. Birmingham's expansive clay is the root cause of most calls. Owning this topic positions Foundation Pro as the local authority.

Recommended format: Regional pillar post with diagrams, soil maps, before/after examples

Suburb-specific landing pages (Hoover, Vestavia, Mountain Brook, Homewood, Trussville) High Priority

Competitors rank for 'foundation repair [suburb]' searches that you don't. Each page should mention local landmarks, neighborhoods, and soil conditions.

Recommended format: Individual location pages with unique content, embedded map, local testimonials

Pier installation: helical vs push piers vs steel piers Medium Priority

Method-specific pages capture homeowners who have already self-diagnosed and are researching solutions.

Recommended format: Educational comparison page with diagrams and cost ranges

Before/after project portfolio with full case studies Medium Priority

Visual proof converts. Most homeowners scrolling want to see actual local jobs, not stock photos.

Recommended format: Portfolio page with location, problem, repair method, photos, customer quote

7 Prioritized Action Plan
Quick Wins do this week — under 2 hours each
  1. Add an H1 tag to the homepage — single most impactful change. Something like 'Birmingham's Family-Owned Foundation Repair & Waterproofing'
  2. Set up info@foundationproservices.com and replace the Gmail address everywhere on the site
  3. Add LocalBusiness schema markup with address, hours, service area, and review count — improves local pack visibility
  4. Add FAQ schema to the 5 service pages that don't have it (foundation repair, basement waterproofing, sump pumps, concrete leveling, exterior water management)
  5. Rewrite the homepage title tag to lead with the keyword: 'Foundation Repair Birmingham AL | Foundation Pro Services'
  6. Reduce H2 count on homepage from 23 down to ~8, demote the rest to H3 — cleaner heading hierarchy
  7. Add a visible address and operating hours block in the footer for consistency with Google Business Profile (NAP)
Strategic Investments plan for this quarter
  1. Publish 8-12 cornerstone blog posts over the next quarter covering signs of foundation problems, repair costs, Alabama clay soil, and seasonal foundation issues
  2. Build out 5-7 suburb-specific landing pages (Hoover, Vestavia Hills, Mountain Brook, Homewood, Trussville, Helena, Pelham) — each with unique local content
  3. Create a project gallery / case studies section with at least 12 real before/after stories — photos, location, problem, solution
  4. Develop method-specific pages for pier installation types (helical, push, steel) with diagrams and comparisons
  5. Optimize for Core Web Vitals — Elementor sites typically need image lazy-loading, font preloading, and JS deferral to hit Google's mobile thresholds
  6. Build a review-collection workflow that auto-requests Google reviews after each completed job — review velocity is a top local-pack ranking factor
