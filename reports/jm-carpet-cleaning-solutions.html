1 Executive Summary
58/100
Critical

J&M Carpet Cleaning Solutions has a clean, friendly homepage and a strong service-area footprint across the Valley — Phoenix, Mesa, Gilbert, Chandler, Tempe, Scottsdale, Glendale and more. The basics are in place: HTTPS, a mobile viewport, LocalBusiness schema markup, and a single H1. The Wix-built site loads, and you have visible reviews, services, and contact details on the homepage.

That said, there is real room to climb in Phoenix search results. Your title tag has a double space and stops short of the keywords most people actually type, the homepage relies on a single long block of text instead of dedicated service pages, and there are no neighborhood or city pages targeting the suburbs you already serve. Fixing these structural gaps and adding a handful of focused pages would put you in front of a meaningfully larger share of local searchers.

6 Content Gap Recommendations

Topics and content types your competitors cover that you're missing.

City and neighborhood landing pages High Priority

You list nine suburbs in your meta description but have no individual page for any of them, leaving easy local rankings on the table

Recommended format: Short landing page per city: services, local landmarks, embedded map, reviews from that area

Standalone service pages High Priority

Carpet, rug, tile and grout, upholstery, and pet treatment each deserve a focused page that ranks for its own keyword set

Recommended format: One page per service with pricing range, process, before / after photos, FAQs

Pet stain and odor education content High Priority

High intent informational searches drive bookings and you currently have no editorial content

Reviews and testimonials hub Medium Priority

7 Prioritized Action Plan
Quick Wins do this week — under 2 hours each
  1. Rewrite homepage title to 'Carpet Cleaning Phoenix AZ | J&M Carpet Cleaning Solutions' and remove the double space
  2. Rewrite the meta description into one benefit-led sentence under 160 characters with a clear call to action
  3. Clean the H1 so it renders as 'J&M Carpet Cleaning Solutions' without the encoded ampersand or trailing space
  4. Add descriptive alt text to the two images currently missing it
  5. Add H3 subheadings under each service block so the page is easier for both readers and search engines to parse
  6. Add a Google Business Profile link in the footer for a stronger NAP signal
  7. Add internal links from each service block to a future standalone service page (placeholder anchors are fine)
Strategic Investments plan for this quarter
  1. Build five standalone service pages: Carpet Cleaning, Rug Cleaning, Tile and Grout, Upholstery, Pet Treatment — each 600 to 900 words with photos, FAQs, and a booking call to action
  2. Build five service-area pages for Mesa, Gilbert, Chandler, Tempe, and Scottsdale with localized content and an embedded map
  3. Launch a small blog with five anchor posts: pet urine removal, how often to clean carpet, tile sealing basics, carpet cleaning vs. replacement, and stain emergency guide
  4. Add a dedicated reviews page that aggregates your Google reviews, with Review schema markup
  5. Migrate or audit the Wix theme to reduce page weight and improve mobile load time
  6. Add FAQ schema to the homepage and any service page that includes Q&A copy
